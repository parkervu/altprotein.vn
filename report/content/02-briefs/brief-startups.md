# Brief for startups and founders

**The short version.** Vietnam offers cheap operating inputs, abundant carbohydrate, co-packers and concentrated feed buyers, but no novel-food route, no open pilot fermenter and very little venture capital. The founders most likely to reach revenue in two years are those who pick a product with a clear route today, sell it to a named business buyer, and borrow capacity rather than build it.

## Pick a product with a route that exists today

| Product | Route | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Plant-based food from known ingredients (textured protein, *chay*) | Self-declaration | Immediate |
| Koji and traditional mould fermentation on food substrates | Self-declaration; keep strain and mycotoxin data | Immediate |
| Insect, duckweed, algae or yeast meal for livestock feed | Listed: factory certificate and conformity declaration | About 35 working days for the factory |
| The same materials in aquafeed | Unclear: ask the Department of Fisheries in writing | Trial may be required |
| Bacterial single-cell protein or new fungal biomass in feed | Trial, recognition, annual list update | About 40 working days plus the trial; our estimate is 6 to 18 months in total |
| Fungal biomass or mycoprotein food | Self-declaration, but no way to get pre-clearance | Immediate, with legal risk |
| Precision-fermented protein made in Vietnam | Closed-production GMO certificate, then a food route | Not published |
| Cultivated meat or seafood | No national route; Hanoi sandbox is the nearest option | Not published |

Source: [[ch05-rules]]. Unlisted feed materials are now fined (VND 10 to 20 million each, plus suspension), so run the trial first.

## Plays that suit founders

- **T1. Textured plant protein.** Buyers are *chay* brands, frozen-food makers and co-packers who now import Chinese textured soy and gluten. Ex-factory cost is roughly 3,700 to 10,700 VND per 100 g of protein, against 34,000 VND or more for eggs and chicken at retail; the competitor is Chinese product at about USD 1.6 per kg ([[ch09-economics]]).
- **T2. Functional microbial feed ingredients.** Sell survival and feed-efficiency gains at 1 to 5% of shrimp or pangasius diets. Get species-specific trial data first; most shrimp feed is made by about six firms ([[ch11-plays]]).
- **T9. Koji and fungal foods.** Use a characterised *Aspergillus oryzae* strain (no cyclopiazonic acid or 3-nitropropionic acid risk) on food-grade side streams ([[ch10-technology-fit]]).
- **T6. Duckweed feed.** Low capital, listed for livestock; test heavy metals and manganese on your water ([[ch10-technology-fit]]).
- **T7. Precision fermentation.** Only high-value, low-dose products, made under contract abroad ([[ch11-plays]]).

## Where to locate

- **Cassava and starch residues:** Tay Ninh (65 to 68 starch factories) and the Central Highlands. Cassava roots rose about 75% in a year; build on residues, not roots ([[ch02-asset-map]], [[app-c-feedstocks]]).
- **Industry, co-packers, parks and labs:** Ho Chi Minh City (including former Binh Duong and Ba Ria-Vung Tau) and Dong Nai. Industrial land about USD 186 per m² for the lease term; ready-built factories about USD 5 per m² a month ([[ch09-economics]], [[app-f-facilities]]).
- **Aquafeed buyers and trials:** the Mekong Delta and Ho Chi Minh City.
- **Sandbox, research institutes, the Hanoi venture fund:** Hanoi ([[ch05-rules]], [[ch07-capital]]).

## Borrow capacity

- **Co-packers:** frozen-food processors already run vegetarian lines next to meat ([[ch03-industrial-base]]).
- **Feed mills:** about 269 mills at roughly half their capacity can host trials ([[ch03-industrial-base]]).
- **Fermentation:** Vedan, Ajinomoto and Daesang own the aerobic tanks; we found no published tolling offer, so ask ([[ch14-unknowns]]). For scale-up, ScaleUp Bio (Singapore, food-licensed, up to 10,000 L), EECi (Thailand, two 15,000 L tanks, food status to confirm) or Chinese CDMOs (largest and cheapest, more exposure of know-how) ([[ch08-regional]]).
- **Testing:** FIRI's NACEFA and NIFC for proximate analysis, contaminants and microbiology. Amino-acid and digestibility testing may need to go abroad ([[app-l-labs-talent]]).

## Design for Vietnam

- **Cooling.** Cooling-tower water in the south arrives at about 31 to 33 °C (our engineering assumption). Chilled water adds roughly USD 70 to 180 per tonne of biomass; thermotolerant strains that grow at 40 to 45 °C avoid most of it ([[ch09-economics]]).
- **Power.** About 1,952 VND per kWh blended at 22 kV for a 24/7 plant, but the grid emits about 0.66 kg CO2 per kWh; a low-carbon claim needs renewable supply ([[ch09-economics]]).
- **VAT.** Feed is not subject to VAT, so a feed-ingredient maker cannot reclaim input VAT; budget 5 to 10% more on taxed inputs ([[ch09-economics]]).
- **Names and addresses.** Use the post-July 2025 province names in licences and investor documents ([[app-p-admin-map]]).

## Money

Angels and family offices; regional seed specialists (USD 0.1 to 0.3 million: Better Bite Ventures, Big Idea Ventures, ProVeg Incubator); ADB Ventures (up to USD 4 million, climate lens); the Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi venture funds from late 2026; episodic international grants. A Singapore holding company, as Entobel used, can ease foreign investment ([[ch07-capital]], [[app-m-capital]]).

## Looking to 2050 (edition 1.1)

- **Windows open in sequence.** Functional feed ingredients, AI-assisted enzymes and tolling on existing tanks now; cheap second-hand tanks in 2027 to 2030; contract precision fermentation and food mycoprotein in 2030 to 2035 once a new-food route exists; residue sugar and protein from power in the 2040s ([[ch16-frontier-technology]]).
- **Edges that survive Chinese price dominance** are species-specific function, local feedstocks and freshness ([[ch18-scenarios-2050]]).
- **Design for the 2050 climate.** Use strains that grow at 40 °C or more, raised Mekong sites with surface water, and residue carbon if you want a climate claim: cassava-based microbial protein is not low-carbon by default.
- **Omega-3 may be the sharper opportunity than protein.** Vietnamese aquafeed needs about 11 kt of EPA plus DHA today and about 21 kt by 2050 (our estimate), and Vietnam's own fish oil is not an omega-3 source ([[ch17-protein-balance-2050]]).
