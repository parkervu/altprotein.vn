# Q. Open questions register

This register lists every question the research could not close. It is generated from `open_questions.csv`, which also holds the source notes each question came from. The twenty questions that matter most, with the play each unblocks, are ranked in [[ch14-unknowns]]; their rank appears in the "Priority" column below. Edition 1.1 adds the questions left by the futures round (OQ-128 onwards, topic "futures 2030 to 2050"); the most important are summarised in [[ch20-robust-moves]].

Status: 133 open; 22 partly closed.

Readers who can answer any of these are invited to contact AltProtein Vietnam; closed questions will be dated in the data file.
