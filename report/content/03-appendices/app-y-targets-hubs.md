# Y. Targets, benchmarks and places: the policy and spatial frame to 2050

**What this appendix contains.** The evidence behind [[ch19-vision-2050]] and [[ch20-robust-moves]]: a register of Vietnam's official targets relevant to protein supply by horizon, what those targets leave out, benchmarks from protein and bioeconomy strategies abroad, the map layers and six candidate hubs for a protein bioeconomy, and the full registers of the 22 vision milestones and the 21 robust moves.

**How to use it.** Policy makers can start with Y.1 and Y.2 (what is planned, what is missing, and the 2026 to 2030 windows to add targets). Investors and founders can start with Y.5 (places and timing). Y.6 and Y.7 are the full tables behind chapters 19 and 20.

> **Method note.** Official targets carry `{fx:projection}`: they are official targets, not forecasts. Hub scores, hub timings and site arithmetic are our estimates and carry no probabilities. The milestones in Y.6 are normative. We read the legal texts on thuvienphapluat.vn, a commercial mirror of official texts; the extraction tool sometimes paraphrased, so quotes are Medium confidence unless confirmed twice. Every "we found no" was checked with explicit keyword searches [@NTS-34] {VN-direct|Medium}. Decision numbers are Prime Minister decisions (QD-TTg) unless stated.

**Reading.** The state plans the **inputs** of a future protein system (clean power, hydrogen, carbon limits, water) to 2050. It plans the **outputs** (meat, feed, aquaculture, feed crops) only to 2030, and it never plans protein itself [@NTS-07; @NTS-08; @NTS-26; @NTS-24] {VN-direct|High}. The first official 2035 numbers relevant to protein supply are Decision 1466's biotech targets.

**What changed in 2025 and 2026.** Decision 1466 replaced the 2021 agri-biotech scheme (Decision 429); Resolution 252 amended the national master plan (Resolution 81/2023); Decision 1177 adjusted the land plan; Decision 604 aligned the science strategy with Resolution 57; Decision 1355/QD-BCT extended MOIT's 2021 biotech scheme to 2045; Decision 768 replaced the 2023 power plan. The livestock, fisheries, crop, climate, methane, hydrogen, water and feed instruments are unchanged, and their MARD and MONRE tasks now sit with MAE [@NTS-35; @NTS-14; @NTS-15; @NTS-21; @NTS-38; @NTS-26] {VN-direct|Medium}.

## Y.2 What is missing

### Y.2.1 Absences

- **No protein, feed or alternative-protein number beyond 2030.** We found no official meat, feed, fishmeal, feed-crop or alternative-protein number for 2035, 2040, 2045 or 2050 in Decisions 1520, 339, 150, 1748, 1625, 1466, 1664 or 389 [@NTS-07; @NTS-08; @NTS-22; @NTS-23; @NTS-35; @AQF-01; @AQF-15] {VN-direct|High}.
- **No aquafeed or fishmeal target at any horizon.** Decisions 339 and 1664 order trash-fish replacement and feed zones without a volume or date [@NTS-08; @AQF-01] {VN-direct|High}.
- **No feed-crop or feed-import target.** The land plan releases about 654 thousand ha of rice land by 2030 without saying for what [@NTS-22; @NTS-15] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **No protein-intake norm.** The nutrition, health and food-systems instruments set no protein target and do not mention plant-based or new foods [@NTS-16; @NTS-17; @NTS-10] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **Fermentation is absent from the new instruments.** The strategic technology missions, Decision 1466, the AI strategy and the draft biotechnology strategy do not name fermentation, microbial biomass or alternative protein [@NTS-20; @NTS-35; @FTB-48; @FTB-49] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **No pilot-plant or biofoundry target, and little money.** Decision 1466 sets labs and staff numbers only. We found no budget line for Resolution 36; the only protein-adjacent budget is the feed scheme's, about USD 6 million a year [@NTS-35; @REG-51; @NTS-23] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **The official livestock climate levers are rations, manure and biogas,** not alternative protein [@NTS-24] {VN-direct|Medium}.

### Y.3.4 Framing and review cycles

- **Security framing has displaced climate framing (2024 to 2026).** The EU's July 2026 plan is titled for "resilience, strategic autonomy and sustainability"; Japan and Korea state targets as food security; Singapore left alternative protein out of its food-security targets for cost and acceptance reasons [@VIS-15; @VIS-13; @VIS-29; @VIS-25] {general|Medium} {fx:signal}.
- **Denmark alone pairs a transition fund with a price on livestock emissions** from 2030. Vietnam's carbon market excludes agriculture, so this is a benchmark for the 2040s, not a near-term lever [@VIS-03; @VIS-04] {general|Medium} {fx:signal}.
- **Review cycles decide whether a vision survives.** Japan checks KPIs every year; Finland reports each electoral term; Korea runs five-year plans under a statute (`foresight_methods.csv`, FSM-012, FSM-017, FSM-018) [@VIS-13; @VIS-44; @VIS-14] {general|Medium}.
- **Vietnam's foresight base is thin.** The Delphi for the 2021 to 2030 science strategy had 15 experts per field; its authors advise small scenario workshops [@VIS-45] {VN-direct|High}. *Vietnam 2035* is the precedent for a jointly written national vision [@VIS-46] {VN-direct|Medium}. ### Y.5.9 A siting rule for 2050, and the scenarios

A siting rule for the S-ALT plants in 2050, not a forecast {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}: plants on sugar follow carbohydrate (CH-3, CH-4, the Central Highlands); plants on hydrogen follow electrolysers (CH-1 first, CH-5 later); functional-ingredient plants follow buyers and trial sites (CH-2, CH-4). No hub can host the whole scenario. In the 2035 scenarios of [[ch13-outlook]], CH-4 alone matters in Scenario 1; CH-2 and CH-3 lead Scenario 2; Scenario 3 needs CH-4 for tolling and CH-6 for research. The hydrogen hubs (CH-1 at scale, CH-5) belong only to 2040 to 2050 worlds in which new-energy offshore wind is built; the mapping to the 2050 worlds is in [[ch18-scenarios-2050]].
