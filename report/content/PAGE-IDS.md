## Corrections that supersede earlier numbers (apply everywhere)
- Fishmeal (World Bank, Peru 65% CIF series): 2025 average USD 1,706/t; August 2026 USD 2,500/t (supersedes USD 1,837/t, March 2026). Per tonne of protein: about USD 2,625 (2025) and USD 3,846 (Aug 2026). Soybean meal about USD 403/t (Aug 2026), about USD 880/t protein.
- Displaceable fishmeal pool in Vietnamese aquafeed: central 120 to 245 kt/yr; sourced bounds 79 to 290 kt; realistic capture by novel proteins in total 25 to 90 kt. The 350 to 450 kt in earlier drafts is not supported.
- Soy (2025): 2.61 Mt soybeans imported (98.4% of supply), 5.70 Mt soybean meal imported, 7.2 Mt soybean meal fed; about 99% of soy protein in feed is imported.
- Livestock and poultry compound feed about 22 Mt (2025); aquafeed estimates 3.9 to 4.8 Mt (industry) vs 6.5 Mt (USDA), unresolved.
- De Heus bought CJ Feed & Care (announced 1 Oct 2025, closed 3 Mar 2026): 17 mills across five countries, not 17 in Vietnam. De Heus Vietnam lists 21 plants. Vinh Long marine feed mill 168,000 t/yr (company) vs 84,000 (trade press).
- Vedan glutamic acid nameplate 180,000 t/yr (2004, last published), not 36,000.
- Skretting and Entobel partnership (26 Nov 2025): first commercial insect-meal shrimp feed in Vietnam (company claim).
- Entobel financing: USD 32.5 M project (Mekong Capital USD 25 M, Dragon Capital USD 5 M, IFC USD 2.5 M upstream collaboration agreement). Not USD 36 M.
- E10 gasoline mandatory nationwide from 1 June 2026 (Circular 50/2025/TT-BCT effective 1 Jan 2026).
- Long An merged into Tay Ninh (not HCMC). Binh Duong and Ba Ria-Vung Tau into HCMC. Binh Phuoc into Dong Nai.
- Feed trials: Law on Animal Husbandry Art 37 and Circular 94/2025 (not Art 11 of Decree 13/2020, which is factory inspection). Agency time about 40 working days plus the trial; total 6 to 18 months is our estimate.
- Aquafeed permitted list (Circular 16/2026/TT-BNNMT Appendix II) has no raw-material section. Decree 211/2026 (from 5 Aug 2026) fines VND 10 to 20 M per unlisted feed raw material plus 1 to 3 months' suspension.
- Precision-fermented proteins are probably a "product of a GMO" under Decree 43/2026 (definition includes "protein from the modified gene").
- Karimi et al. 2019 "118.5 g/L A. oryzae" is not a broth concentration (likely per litre of undiluted vinasse, about 5.9 g/L in the flask). Do not use as a titer.
- ICAR-NBFGR PHT striped catfish line is real (NRFC-078) but a serum-dependent virology line. PmLyO-Sf9 is a black tiger shrimp x insect hybrid from CUSAT, not ICAR-NBFGR.
- EFSA (2021) could not establish safety of whole Wolffia globosa powder (manganese); found a Lemna protein concentrate safe (2023). "PDCAAS 0.89" for duckweed is unsupported and must not be used.
- ">50 g/L titer" (Nielsen et al., Annu Rev Food Sci Technol 2024) is an expert assertion; best leghemoglobin titers 3.5, 7.27 and 10.1 g/L.
- Region I minimum wage 2026: VND 5,310,000/month (Decree 293/2025), not 4.96 M.
- Vietnamese plant-based-meat research: about 5 on-topic papers (2015 to 2026) and no Vietnamese extrusion study (v0.2: the one candidate, vvae080, is Taiwanese).

## Decisions that apply everywhere (from v0.2)
- The 2030 industrial feed target (30 to 32 Mt) covers livestock feed only, per Decision 1625/QD-TTg (Medium confidence); the model gives 26.4 Mt of livestock feed in 2030.
- Decision 1466/QD-TTg (31 July 2026) replaces Decision 429/QD-TTg. The green growth strategy is Decision 1658/QD-TTg; Decision 882 is its action plan.
- Research intensity against Thailand: "about a quarter to a third".
- Soybean-meal need: 7.2 Mt (2025); 10.4 Mt (2050, trend); 7.0 to 11.7 Mt across scenarios; credible ambitious range 7.0 to 8.8 Mt (vision "7 to 9 Mt"). S-ALT is a stretch above every real-world analogue.
- Cheaper capital (12% to 9% charge) does as much as the cost learning expected by 2030, not "a decade of learning".
- 2050 scenarios: A Regional workshop, B Comfortable price-taker, C Security build-out, D Squeezed importer. 2035 scenarios are Scenarios 1 to 3 in chapter 19.
- Gas-route electricity: the model uses 41 kWh per kg of protein; the 2030 plant design in chapter 21 uses about 69 kWh per kg (logged as DG-143).

## Decisions that apply everywhere (from v0.3)
- Demand claims carry a demand evidence tag: {dx:stated}, {dx:revealed}, {dx:tested} or {dx:inferred}. Follow revealed evidence where it disagrees with stated evidence.
- Published Vietnamese plant-based or vegetarian market sizes ("VND 1,000 billion", "USD 500 million", "USD 1 billion", "USD 103 to 112 million") are not used; "about 10% of Vietnamese are vegetarian" is not used.
- The GFI APAC 2023 survey's Vietnam sample (n = 1,049) is an online panel in the Hanoi region and Ho Chi Minh City. "5% to 63%" and "93% interested in blends" are regional stated figures, not forecasts.
- Price benchmarks (24 September 2026, per 100 g of protein): eggs and chicken VND 34,000 to 45,000; lean pork about 70,900; tofu about 30,000 (composition table) or 66,000 (audit labels); imported analogues 542,000 to 622,000 (12 to 18 times eggs and chicken).
- Existing chay days avoid about 25,000 t of meat protein a year (2025; about 2.5% of meat protein; our estimate on a stated 30% keeping 2.5 days a month). They are in the meat baseline and are not counted as displacement.
- Demand model scenarios are D-DRIFT, D-BENCH and D-STRETCH; S-ALT's food shares are read as exploratory. D-BENCH 2035: about 19 kt of protein delivered, about 2.4 kt of meat protein displaced (0.18%).
- The research-institutions table stays `institutions.csv`; Part III meal programmes are in `meal_programmes.csv`.

## Decisions that apply everywhere (from v0.4)
- The demand model is frozen at v0.3 numbers. Where a headline is restated, write "about 19,000 t of protein a year by 2035 on the benchmark path, of which about 11,000 to 17,000 t made in Vietnam". School protein in the plant slot displaces tofu, not meat (D-BENCH 2035 displacement about 2.29 kt, still "about 0.2%"); the food pool is nearer 35 kt than 40 kt; R7 could be 0.5 to 1.2 kt.
- `plays.csv` scores stay; proposed score changes are text only (`play_demand_check.csv`, column `note_v0_4`). T1 is a debt-financed import-substitution plant with no tariff shelter (0% under ACFTA and RCEP); the benchmark path supports about one 1 t/h soy extrusion line by 2035; kill test: food-grade defatted soy flour above about USD 700 to 940 per t delivered. T2 is judged against the about 9,200 t of inactive yeast Vietnam imports for food and feed, 95% of it at about USD 1.1 per kg.
- Blends are priced against hydrated soy, not lean meat; caps are 10 to 20% in fine emulsions and 30% in coarse mince; avoid *độn* and "x% meat replaced" wording.
- Schools: Decision 3958/QĐ-BYT asks for a plant-protein dish at least twice a week and audited menus fill it with tofu; do not start in schools with a branded or meat-replacement product.
- Food Safety Law: first comments at the October 2026 session, passage considered at the May 2027 session.
- Labels: Decree 37/2026 replaced Decree 43/2017 and Circular 30/2026/TT-BYT replaced Circular 29/2023; TCVN 13625:2023 already defines vegetarian and vegan claims; named ingredients must already be quantified; the GMO label applies above 5% of ingredients.
- Sugary-drink excise: 8% (2027) and 10% (2028) above 5 g sugar per 100 ml; dairy exempt; plant milks unresolved (DMV-20).
- Funders: only 4 of the original 19 demand moves have a funder that fits and accepts Vietnam; in funder units the benchmark path spares about 2.4 to 2.7 million animals a year by 2035 and avoids about 0.14 Mt CO2e.
- Startups: aim first for ingredient, private-label or co-packing supply to incumbents; a restaurant listing for your own analogue brand is not a business customer.
- Stunting: 18% (2023), 32% among ethnic minority children; for school-age children protein is not the gap.
- TPP-11 (functional microbial feed ingredient, T2) is added; the zinc-fortified soy dish is withdrawn and mentioned only as a research option.

## Decisions that apply everywhere (from v0.5)
- The prologue is background from global evidence and changes no finding. Where it restates a Vietnamese number, the chapter it cites is authoritative.
- Livestock emissions: give both FAO figures, about 12% (6.2 Gt CO2e, reference year 2015) and 14.5% (7.1 Gt, reference year 2005); the two use different reference years and model versions, so the lower figure is not evidence that emissions fell.
- Footprints per 100 g of protein are Poore and Nemecek (2018) global means as published by Our World in Data; say "means" and note that medians are lower.
- Alternative protein investment: USD 881 million in 2025 and USD 359 million in the first half of 2026 (GFI, advocacy); the 2021 peak is about USD 7 billion in GFI's current series (USD 5.0 billion as first reported).
- Protein demand does not "double by 2050": OECD-FAO project 6% more animal-source calories per person from 2025 to 2034 (24% in lower-middle-income countries), and FAO about 20% more land-animal products from the 2020s to 2050.
