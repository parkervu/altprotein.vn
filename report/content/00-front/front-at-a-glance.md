## What could be done ([[ch11-plays]], [[ch12-policy-options]])

| Horizon | Businesses (plays) | Public steps |
|---|---|---|
| Now to 2028 | T1 domestic textured plant protein; T2 functional microbial feed ingredients; T4 cassava-starch-to-sugar hub (if a fermenter commits); pilots of koji (T9) and duckweed feed (T6) | New-food clause in the Food Safety Law (expected October to November 2026); note on precision-fermented proteins; aquafeed and livestock feed-list fixes |
| 2028 to 2031 | T5 shared pilot and tolling fermentation; T10 mung bean and rice protein; T7 contract-made precision fermentation; T3 bulk fishmeal replacement if costs fall | Novel-food decree with reliance lane; public feed trials; protein-quality laboratory; capital grants over tax holidays |
| 2031 onward | T8 cultivated seafood if a food cell line exists | Regional reliance on novel-food approvals |
