# REG2 (wave 2): closing regulatory gaps for new proteins in Vietnam (as of 23 September 2026)

Agent: REG2 (wave 2). Source prefix: REG2. This file builds on the wave 1 notes (wave1/regulation/regulation.md, prefix REG) and does not repeat them. Evidence labels: [VN-direct], [VN-adjacent], [general]. Confidence: High = primary legal text read by this agent through the fetch tool (extracts, not full verbatim text); Medium = reputable secondary, or primary text read only in part; Low = background knowledge not verified in this wave.

Method note. Wave 2 had no web search. We fetched known URLs and used site-internal search on thuvienphapluat.vn and baochinhphu.vn, and the World Bank WITS TRAINS API. Blocked or empty: docs.wto.org (robots.txt), ePing (JavaScript only), duthaoonline.quochoi.vn and quochoi.vn search (empty pages), EUR-Lex (text not rendered), Thai FDA PDFs (robots.txt), WITS for 2024 (HTTP 404). Large appendices on thuvienphapluat (EIA thresholds, MFN tariff schedule, parts of Decree 43/2026 and Decree 320/2025) were truncated by the fetch tool.

Terms used (in addition to wave 1):
- Permitted list: the Ministry list of feed raw materials or substances that may be used without a feeding trial.
- Closed-production certificate: the new MAE certificate that a genetically modified organism (GMO) may be used in contained ("khép kín") production (Decree 69/2010 Art 37a, added by Decree 43/2026).
- Sandbox (controlled testing, "thử nghiệm có kiểm soát"): a time-limited, supervised permission to test a product or model that current law does not cover.
- MFN: most-favoured-nation (standard) import duty. ATIGA: ASEAN Trade in Goods Agreement. ACFTA: ASEAN-China Free Trade Area.

## Headline findings

1. **The draft Food Safety Law still has no novel-food concept in any public summary.** Government press on the July 2026 draft describes two product groups: (1) foods that must register a product declaration or declare their applied standard ("công bố tiêu chuẩn áp dụng") before sale, and (2) foods that need no declaration. Both are under post-market surveillance. Registration is named for medical nutrition foods, special-diet foods, products for children up to 36 months and health supplements. No summary mentions new food, new ingredients, history of use or a sandbox. We could not read the draft text or the WTO attachment. [VN-direct] Medium. REG2-05, REG2-06, REG2-04.
2. **Timing is unchanged but still not confirmed by a National Assembly source.** A trade source gives submission in October 2026 and entry into force on 1 January 2027 (WTO G/SPS/N/VNM/187, comments closed 17 August 2026). The draft was due to the Government by 10 August 2026. [VN-direct] Medium. REG2-04, REG2-05.
3. **The drafters rejected the "register everything" model.** They called mandatory registration of conformity for all pre-packaged processed foods (the approach of the suspended Decree 46/2026) unsuited to the new management method. This lowers the risk that plant-based and fermented foods face a slow pre-approval step from 2027. [VN-direct] Medium. REG2-06.
4. **The aquafeed permitted list has no raw-material section.** Appendix II of Circular 16/2026/TT-BNNMT is titled as a list of "chemicals, biological products, microorganisms and feed raw materials". It only contains chemicals and biological items: 17 bioactives and extracts, 30 microorganisms, vitamins and enzymes. Insect meal, yeast protein, single-cell protein (SCP), microalgae, duckweed and fermented soybean meal are not listed as raw materials. [VN-direct] High. REG2-01.
5. **So the legal status of new protein raw materials in shrimp and fish feed is ambiguous.** Law on Fisheries Art 35(1) requires a trial when aquafeed contains a "raw material for producing aquafeed not on the list". Read literally, with no raw materials on the list, this catches every raw material, including fishmeal. Practice evidently differs. A startup cannot know from the texts whether insect meal or SCP needs an aquafeed trial. [VN-direct] High (text); Medium (interpretation). REG2-01, REG2-02.
6. **Some microbes that underpin protein ingredients are already on the aquafeed list, but only as microorganisms.** The list includes Candida utilis (Torula yeast), Pichia farinosa, Saccharomyces cerevisiae and boulardii, Aspergillus oryzae and niger, and about 20 Bacillus species. It also lists beta-glucan and chitosan. It has no methanotrophic bacteria (e.g. Methylococcus) and no microalgae. [VN-direct] High. REG2-01.
7. **The livestock permitted list is unchanged since February 2023.** Circular 21/2019 Appendix VI, as amended by Circular 31/2022, is still the list. Circular 94/2025 repeals only parts of Circular 22/2019 and Circular 21/2024 and has no list of its own. We found no 2024 to 2026 amendment adding "protein đơn bào" (single-cell protein) or "sinh khối vi sinh vật" (microbial biomass). [VN-direct] High on repeal scope; Medium on absence. REG2-08, REG2-09, REG2-07.
8. **Using an unlisted feed raw material is now expensive.** Decree 211/2026/ND-CP took effect on 5 August 2026. It fines VND 10 to 15 million for each type of unlisted raw material used, or VND 15 to 20 million at higher values. It adds a 1 to 3 month suspension of feed production and destruction of the material. For a new SCP or fungal biomass, the feed trial (or a list update) is now clearly worth its cost. [VN-direct] High. REG2-10.
9. **The closed-production GMO certificate has real conditions but no visible timeline.** The strain must come from safe donor and recipient organisms and have a safety history in contained use under OECD or G20 standards. The facility must prevent release and hold an environmental licence for waste treatment. The dossier is Form 32 (application) and Form 33 (technical description), filed with MAE. The appraisal time, validity and fee were not visible. The MAE procedure list (Decision 375/QĐ-BNNMT) may place it at provincial level, which conflicts with the decree. [VN-direct] High on conditions; Low on authority level. REG2-11, REG2-12.
10. **No rule covers products made with GMOs that contain no GMO.** Decree 43/2026 only defines "product of a GMO" as containing GM genetic material "or protein from the modified gene". A purified recombinant protein still fits that definition, so the wave 1 reading stands: labelling above 5% applies; whether a food-use certificate is also needed is unanswered. [VN-direct] Medium. REG2-11.
11. **There is still no national sandbox procedure, but Hanoi has one that names biotech.** Law 93/2025 Art 21 leaves approval to "the competent state agency" and lets the Government issue detailed rules (Art 21(6)). The four decrees guiding that law (263, 265, 267 and 268/2025/ND-CP, 14 October 2025) do not set up controlled testing. Hanoi does: Resolution 29/2025/NQ-HĐND sets the procedure under the Capital Law, and Decision 6150/QĐ-UBND (11 December 2025) lists biotech, advanced bio-medical technology and high-tech agriculture as priority fields. The Hanoi People's Committee approves. Hai Phong and Da Nang have similar schemes. [VN-direct] High (Hanoi list); Medium (Hanoi procedure not read). REG2-13 to REG2-17.
12. **Two CIT tests in Decree 320/2025 matter for alt protein.** First, the processing exemption needs agricultural or aquatic raw materials of at least 30% of production cost. A cassava-based fermentation plant likely passes; a plant running on imported refined sugar may not (our interpretation). Second, products made with technology applied for the first time in Vietnam get 3 years of tax exemption, once an authority confirms the technology is new. We could not read the high-tech project criteria. [VN-direct] Medium. REG2-18.
13. **Feed protein imports are duty-free, so domestic novel proteins get no tariff shelter.** Decree 73/2025 cut MFN duty on feed maize (1005.90.99) from 2% to 0% and on soybean meal (2304.00.29, 2304.00.90) to 0% from 31 March 2025. Soybeans (1201.90) and fishmeal (2301.20) were already 0% in WITS 2023 data. [VN-direct] Medium. REG2-21, REG2-23.
14. **Food protein ingredients carry small MFN duties.** WITS 2023 applied rates are: wheat gluten (1109) 10%; protein concentrates and textured protein (2106.10) 5%; peptones and other proteins (3504) 3%; active yeast (2102.10) 10%; inactive yeast and other dead single-cell organisms (2102.20) 7%. Defatted soy flour for food (2304.00.21) stayed at 2%. We did not verify 2026 values or ATIGA and ACFTA rates. [VN-direct] Medium. REG2-20, REG2-21, REG2-23.
15. **Vietnam has no mandatory allergen list and no rule on plant-based names.** Neither Decree 111/2021 nor Circular 05/2019/TT-BKHCN, in the extracts we read, lists allergens or regulates "chay", "thuần chay", "thịt" (meat) or "sữa" (milk) on plant-based products. Labels only require generic "warning information". [VN-direct] Medium (absence). REG2-24, REG2-25.
16. **Thailand shows what a working, proportionate route looks like.** The Thai FDA runs novel food under MOPH Notification No. 376 (2016). Safety assessment is done by FDA-recognised assessment units. There are separate forms for cultivated meat and for "non-novel" foods, a mycoprotein guideline, and an approved list updated in September 2025. Vietnam could copy the assessment-unit model by decree. [VN-adjacent] Medium. REG2-26.
17. **EIA thresholds for food, feed and fermentation plants remain unknown.** The appendices of Decree 08/2022 and Decree 05/2025 did not render, as in wave 1. Decree 05/2025 moved some EIA appraisal and permitting to provinces from 6 January 2025. [VN-direct] Gap. REG2-19.

## Detailed findings

### Q1. Draft revised Food Safety Law (2026)

**What we could and could not read.** The WTO notification G/SPS/N/VNM/187 is on docs.wto.org, which blocks automated fetching (robots.txt). The ePing search page loads its results with JavaScript. The National Assembly draft portal and quochoi.vn search returned empty pages. thuvienphapluat search found only the MOH drafting plan (Decision 1834/QĐ-BYT, 3 June 2025) and no draft text. So the draft itself was not read. [VN-direct] Gap. REG2-07.

**What the Government's own press says about the July 2026 draft:**
- It has four policy groups: reform of the management approach, stronger post-market control, digital transformation, and (from wave 1) street food and catering. REG2-05.
- Product declaration: "nhóm thực phẩm phải đăng ký bản công bố hoặc công bố tiêu chuẩn áp dụng trước khi lưu hành" (foods that must register a declaration or declare the applied standard before circulation) and "nhóm thực phẩm không phải thực hiện công bố" (foods that need no declaration). Both groups are inspected and monitored "throughout circulation on the market". REG2-05, REG2-06.
- The registration group named in the press is: medical nutrition foods, foods for special diets, nutrition products for children up to 36 months, and health supplements ("thực phẩm bảo vệ sức khỏe"). REG2-06.
- The drafters rejected requiring all pre-packaged processed foods to register a conformity declaration ("đăng ký bản công bố hợp quy"), as "not suited to the management method". REG2-06.
- Advertising of medical nutrition foods and products for children up to 36 months would be banned. REG2-06.
- The draft was to be finalised by 10 August 2026 for submission to the Government. REG2-05.

**What this means for new proteins.** Ordinary plant-based and fermented protein foods would fall in either the "declare the applied standard" group or the "no declaration" group. Which one is not clear from the press. Neither involves pre-approval. So the new law, as described, keeps an open door but also keeps the uncertainty: there is still no way to get a new ingredient positively cleared. We do not know whether the recall ground for "new-technology food not yet permitted" (current Law Art 55(1)(c)) is carried over. [VN-direct] Medium (interpretation).

**Timing.** GPC Gateway: 71 articles in 8 chapters; submission to the National Assembly in October 2026; entry into force 1 January 2027; WTO comments closed 17 August 2026. A National Assembly deputy (wave 1, REG-14) said the law will be reviewed at the second 2026 session. We found no National Assembly agenda document confirming whether the law will be passed at one session or two. [VN-direct] Medium. REG2-04.

### Q2. Aquafeed permitted list (Circular 16/2026/TT-BNNMT, Appendix II)

**Structure of Appendix II** (title: "Danh mục hoá chất, chế phẩm sinh học, vi sinh vật, nguyên liệu sản xuất thức ăn được phép sử dụng trong nuôi trồng thuỷ sản tại Việt Nam"):
- I. Chemicals: 1 minerals for aquafeed and environment products; 2 chemicals for environment treatment; 3 preservatives, antioxidants and other substances.
- II. Biological products, microorganisms, vitamins, amino acids: 1 bioactives and extracts from organisms (17 entries); 2 microorganisms (30 entries); 3 vitamins; 4 enzymes.
- There is no raw-material section. [VN-direct] High. REG2-01.

**Entries relevant to new proteins:**

| Candidate | On the aquafeed list? | Entry (verbatim where available) |
|---|---|---|
| Insect meal (BSF larvae) | No | none |
| Yeast protein, inactive yeast | Only as microorganisms | II.2 no. 8 "Candida utilis"; no. 22 "Pichia farinosa"; no. 27 "Saccharomyces (Saccharomyces boulardii, Saccharomyces cerevisiae)" |
| Yeast derivatives | Yes, as bioactives | II.1 no. 11 "β-Glucan (beta-glucan)" |
| Bacterial SCP (e.g. Methylococcus) | No | none |
| Microalgae (Spirulina, Chlorella, Schizochytrium) | No | none |
| Duckweed ("bèo") | No | none |
| Fermented soybean meal | No (the fermenting microbes are listed) | II.2 no. 3 "Aspergillus (Aspergillus niger, Aspergillus oryzae)"; no. 4 Bacillus spp. incl. B. subtilis, B. natto, B. licheniformis, B. amyloliquefaciens |
| Chitin derivatives (insect or shrimp shell) | Yes, as bioactives | II.1 no. 4 "Chitosan-oligosaccharide"; no. 8 "Low-molecular-weight Chitosan" |

[VN-direct] High. REG2-01.

**Legal consequence.** Law on Fisheries Art 35(1): aquafeed must be trialled "trong trường hợp có hóa chất, chế phẩm sinh học, vi sinh vật, nguyên liệu sản xuất thức ăn thủy sản không có tên trong danh mục quy định" (where it contains chemicals, biological products, microorganisms or aquafeed raw materials not on the prescribed list). REG2-02. Circular 16/2026 Art 12(2) says the list is Appendix II. The body of the circular does not say that livestock-list materials, or conventional materials such as fishmeal and soybean meal, count as permitted. Art 19(1)(d) makes DFFI responsible for reviewing and updating the list, with no stated way for firms to propose additions. Art 18(2)(c) lets firms import feed for research with an approved research outline. [VN-direct] High (text). REG2-01.

Our reading: either (a) the regulator treats conventional raw materials as outside the list requirement by practice, in which case insect meal and yeast protein probably are too, or (b) any unlisted raw material formally needs a trial. The texts do not settle this. A written DFFI opinion is the cheapest fix. [VN-direct] Medium (interpretation).

Law 146/2025/QH15's changes to Fisheries Law Art 31 to 36 were not visible in the extract. REG2-03.

### Q3. Livestock feed permitted list

- **Still Circular 21/2019 Appendix VI as amended by Circular 31/2022.** Circular 94/2025 Art 18 repeals only Art 4, 5 and 6 of Circular 22/2019 (breeds) and Art 1(2) of Circular 21/2024. It has no raw-material list. REG2-08. Circular 21/2024/TT-BNNPTNT (11 December 2024) amends Circular 22/2019, not 21/2019. REG2-09. [VN-direct] High.
- **No 2024 to 2026 additions found.** thuvienphapluat search for the list title returned the Law on Animal Husbandry, QCVN amendment Circular 05/2021, Circular 66/2025 (other farmed animals) and Decree 211/2026, but no amending circular for Appendix VI. The Appendix VI extract shows microorganisms such as Lactobacillus farciminis, Pediococcus acidilactici and Saccharomyces cerevisiae boulardii. It has no "protein đơn bào" or "sinh khối" entry. [VN-direct] Medium (absence; search listings can miss documents). REG2-07, REG2-09.
- **Law 146/2025 and Art 37.** The extract shows amended Art 37 wording on the Minister issuing the list of chemicals, biological products and microorganisms banned in feed. The trial trigger for a "new substance" was not visible in the amended text. [VN-direct] Low to Medium. REG2-03.
- **New penalties (Decree 211/2026/ND-CP, 16 June 2026, effective 5 August 2026).** Art 22(1): "Phạt tiền từ 10.000.000 đồng đến 15.000.000 đồng đối với hành vi sử dụng mỗi loại nguyên liệu không có trong Danh mục nguyên liệu được phép sử dụng" (fine of VND 10 to 15 million for using each type of raw material not in the permitted list), when the goods are worth under VND 200 million or illicit profit is under VND 100 million. Art 22(2): VND 15 to 20 million at higher values. Additional penalty: suspension of feed production for 1 to 3 months. Remedies: surrender of illicit profit and destruction of the material. [VN-direct] High. REG2-10.

### Q4. Decree 43/2026/ND-CP: closed-production certificate (Art 37a) and GMO-derived products

- **Conditions (Art 37a(2) and (3)).** The GMO comes from safe donor and recipient organisms and has a safety history in closed production under OECD or G20 standards. The facility is a closed system that prevents release into the environment, has waste and emission treatment licensed by the environmental authority, and operates to OECD or G20 closed-production standards. [VN-direct] High. REG2-11.
- **Dossier and filing (Art 37a(4)).** Application form (Mẫu 32) and technical description (Mẫu 33). Filing in person, by post or online with MAE. Completeness check at once for in-person filing and within 1 working day otherwise; incomplete dossiers are refused in writing. MAE issues, re-issues and revokes certificates. [VN-direct] High. REG2-11.
- **Timeline, council, validity, fee.** Not visible: the page was cut off after Art 37a(4)(b), and the procedure page (Decision 375/QĐ-BNNMT, 29 January 2026) did not show steps or fees. [VN-direct] Gap. REG2-11, REG2-12.
- **Which level issues it?** Decree text: MAE. The extract of Decision 375 placed the new procedure in a provincial-level section (Department of Agriculture and Environment). We treat the decree as authoritative; the listing may reflect delegation or an extraction error. [VN-direct] Low. REG2-12.
- **Food/feed GMO certificate.** Decision 375 shows the procedure amended by Decree 43/2026 and implemented by the MAE Department of Science and Technology (Vụ Khoa học và Công nghệ). Timelines from wave 1 (27 or 77 working days of agency steps) stand. REG2-12.
- **Products derived from GMOs that are not GMOs.** No article found. The only relevant text is the Art 5 definition: "Sản phẩm của sinh vật biến đổi gen là sản phẩm được tạo ra từ sinh vật biến đổi gen có chứa vật liệu di truyền hoặc protein từ gen biến đổi" (a product of a GMO is a product created from a GMO that contains GM genetic material or protein from the modified gene). A precision-fermented protein is itself "protein from the modified gene". So it is a product of a GMO, but the certificate procedures are written for organisms. [VN-direct] High (text); Medium (interpretation). REG2-11.

### Q5. Law 93/2025/QH15 controlled testing (sandbox)

- **Law text.** Art 21(1): the "cơ quan nhà nước có thẩm quyền" (competent state agency) permits testing; no single body is named. Art 21(3): the plan must set objectives, conditions and criteria for the technology or solution, risk management and participant protection. Duration: proposed by the firm, up to 3 years, extendable once by up to 3 years. Art 22: regulators and implementers are exempt from civil, administrative and criminal liability if they follow the rules, unless they knew or should have known of a risk and did not prevent it; implementers remain liable to third parties. Art 21(6): the Government may issue detailed rules, including for sectors and for conflicts with other laws. [VN-direct] High. REG2-13.
- **Implementing decrees.** Four decrees of 14 October 2025 guide the law: 263/2025 (autonomy, human resources, talent), 265/2025 (finance and investment), 267/2025 (programmes, tasks, research promotion) and 268/2025 (innovation tasks, loan interest support, vouchers, startups). Decree 268 has no controlled-testing provisions. We found no general sandbox decree. [VN-direct] High (Decree 268); Medium (others by title). REG2-14, REG2-15.
- **Sandboxes that exist.** Banking (Decree 94/2025/ND-CP, 29 April 2025). Hanoi: Resolution 29/2025/NQ-HĐND (29 September 2025) sets criteria and the procedure for registration, appraisal, permission, adjustment, extension and termination; Decision 6150/QĐ-UBND (11 December 2025) lists priority fields, including "công nghệ sinh học" (biotech), "công nghệ y - sinh học tiên tiến" (advanced bio-medical technology) and "công nghệ cao trong nông nghiệp" (high-tech agriculture). The legal basis is the Law on the Capital 2024, Art 25(9). The Hanoi People's Committee approves. Hai Phong (Decision 248/2025/QĐ-UBND, 26 December 2025) and Da Nang (Resolution 20/2026/NQ-HĐND, 29 May 2026) have technology sandboxes. [VN-direct] High (Hanoi list); Medium (others). REG2-16, REG2-17.
- **Process for a food product today.** There is no national route. The practical path is a Hanoi sandbox application under the biotech field, with MOH/VFA consulted. Whether a city sandbox can set aside national food safety rules has not been tested. [VN-direct] Medium (interpretation).

### Q6. Decree 320/2025/ND-CP (CIT)

- Issued 15 December 2025; guides CIT Law 67/2025; applies from the 2025 tax period. [VN-direct] High. REG2-18.
- Art 4(1)(b): processed agricultural and aquatic products qualify for the exemption only if "tỷ lệ giá trị nguyên vật liệu là nông sản, thủy sản trên chi phí sản xuất ... từ 30% trở lên" (agricultural or aquatic raw materials are at least 30% of production cost), and the product is not subject to special consumption tax. [VN-direct] High. REG2-18.
- Art 4(4)(b): "Thu nhập từ bán sản phẩm làm ra từ công nghệ mới lần đầu tiên áp dụng ở Việt Nam được miễn thuế 03 năm" (income from products made with technology applied for the first time in Vietnam is exempt for 3 years), subject to confirmation by the competent authority. This could suit a first precision-fermentation or gas-fermentation plant. [VN-direct] High. REG2-18.
- The incentive chapter (10% for 15 years; 4-year exemption plus 9 years at 50% for "new projects applying high technology on the priority list") did not render. The certification criteria therefore remain unread. [VN-direct] Gap.

### Q7. EIA thresholds

Not closed. The appendices of Decree 08/2022 and Decree 05/2025 did not render, as in wave 1. The fetch tool confirmed only that Appendix II classifies activities by capacity (large, medium, small) and that Group I projects need an EIA. Thread stopped after two attempts. [VN-direct] Gap. REG2-19.

### Q8. Labelling

- Decree 111/2021 (amending Decree 43/2017): food labels need "thông tin cảnh báo" (warning information) under Appendix I item 2, with no definition of its content and no allergen list. No rule on product names for plant-based foods, "chay" or "thuần chay", or use of "thịt" or "sữa". [VN-direct] Medium (extract-based absence). REG2-24.
- Circular 05/2019/TT-BKHCN (26 June 2019; no replacement found): Art 9 covers technical specifications and warnings; no allergen list visible. [VN-direct] Medium. REG2-25.
- Background, not verified in this wave: Decree 43/2017 Art 8 says a product name must not mislead about the nature and use of the goods. Low.
- So Vietnam has no mandatory allergen declaration list of the Codex type (gluten cereals, crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soybeans, milk, tree nuts, sulphites). This matters for soy, wheat-gluten and insect-based foods (insects can cross-react with crustacean allergy). [VN-direct] Medium.

## Implications by audience

- **VCs and investors**
  - The 2027 food law, as described, keeps an open door (no pre-approval for ordinary foods) but offers no positive clearance for new ingredients. Price it as enforcement-discretion risk, not a ban.
  - For shrimp and fish feed plays, ask the company for a written DFFI opinion on whether its ingredient needs an aquafeed trial. The list alone cannot answer this.
  - For precision fermentation made in Vietnam, the closed-production certificate has no published timeline. Treat it as a critical-path item.
- **Policy makers**
  - A one-line delegation clause in the new law (option 1) costs little and prevents a four-to-five-year gap until the next revision.
  - The aquafeed list's missing raw-material section is a drafting gap that one circular can fix.
  - Decree 211/2026 fines make the annual livestock-list update a real bottleneck; adding generic microbial-biomass entries removes it.
- **Startups**
  - Hanoi's sandbox names biotech. It is the only concrete route found for a cultivated-meat or precision-fermentation tasting pilot.
  - Using an unlisted feed ingredient now risks VND 10 to 20 million per material and a 1 to 3 month production suspension. Run the trial first.
  - A first-in-Vietnam technology can claim a 3-year CIT exemption on its products (Decree 320/2025 Art 4(4)(b)), once the novelty is confirmed.
- **F&B and feed manufacturers**
  - Soybean meal and feed maize have been duty-free since March 2025. Novel proteins must beat duty-free incumbents on price.
  - Voluntary allergen labelling (soy, wheat, and a crustacean cross-reactivity warning for insect foods) is prudent because the law is silent.
- **Academia**
  - State-funded feed trials of SCP in shrimp and pangasius are exempt from repeat trials and would feed directly into list updates.
- **International bodies**
  - Technical help on a Thai-style recognised-assessment-unit model and a reliance lane fits the 2026 to 2027 decree drafting window.
