# E-commerce as revealed demand: what online sales counters show

Line: W8-L5 E-commerce as revealed demand (wave 8). Source prefix: ECR. Date: 25 September 2026.

## Verdict

The test this line was set up to run cannot be run with desk tools. Shopee, Lazada, Tiki and TikTok Shop, where Vietnamese shoppers see *đã bán* (units sold) counters, returned a script-only page, a captcha, HTTP 403 and a robots.txt refusal. The grocery chains' web shops (Bach Hoa Xanh, WinMart, Co.op Online) show prices but no sales counters, and Co.op Online's rating fields read zero even for Fami soy milk. Metric.vn's free posts publish liquid-milk and grocery totals but no plant-milk, *chay* (Buddhist vegetarian food), plant-based or protein-powder figures, and YouNet publishes nothing relevant. The counters we could read, on a business-to-business kitchen platform (Kamereo), one mother-and-baby chain (AVAKids) and one sports-nutrition shop (WheyShop), agree with Part VI rather than change it: kitchens buy tofu and dairy milk at scale, plant milks are about 4% of milk litres, imported analogues barely move, and a protein claim does not visibly lift sales. Yield class: **clean negative** on marketplace SKU data (a paid report or a browser-based collector is needed), **noise** for the ch23 consumer picture, TPP-02, TPP-05 and T10, and one **moderate update** to ch25: food e-commerce is growing fast but is an incumbents' channel.

## Headline findings

1. **The four big marketplaces cannot be read with the tools available.** Shopee returned a JavaScript shell with no products; Lazada a captcha redirect; Tiki HTTP 403 on search and category pages; TikTok Shop is disallowed by robots.txt; Sendo's robots.txt failed. We did not work round any of these [VN-direct, High, ECR-11; ECR-12; ECR-13; ECR-14; ECR-15, revealed].
2. **Grocery chains' web shops show no sales counters.** Bach Hoa Xanh lists prices only and forbids its search page to crawlers; WinMart's category pages carry no product data; Co.op Online has a rating block, but every plant-milk page checked read 0 reviews [VN-direct, High, ECR-09; ECR-10; ECR-16, revealed]. Ratings and reviews on retailer sites are therefore not a demand signal in Vietnam today.
3. **Published e-commerce data give totals, not the categories Part VI needs.** Grocery and food on the four marketplaces sold VND 19.55 trillion in the first half of 2026, up 151% on a year earlier and 6.7% of marketplace sales; the leading brands were Vinamilk, Ensure, TH True Milk, Trung Nguyen and Milo. None of Metric's 80 free posts (about September 2024 to September 2026) gives a plant-milk, chay, plant-based or protein-powder figure, and Metric does not describe its method [VN-direct, Medium, ECR-02; ECR-04, revealed].
4. **Online milk is an incumbents' market.** Liquid milk on Shopee sold VND 561.7 billion from 1 January to 28 March 2026 (up 62%); Vinamilk took 53.6% and TH True Milk 16.6%; official brand stores took 84.7% of sales while the number of shops fell 17% [VN-direct, Medium, ECR-01, revealed].
5. **Kitchens buy dairy milk, not plant milk.** On Kamereo, a platform supplying restaurants and cafes, plant milks account for about 4.1% of the 899,000 litres sold across 54 milk SKUs and 1.5 to 2.1% of the milk protein; one pasteurised Dalat Milk 950 ml SKU shows 451,038 units (our calculation on cumulative counters) [VN-direct, Low, ECR-06; FORM-01; DIE-12, revealed].
6. **The plant milk kitchens buy is imported oat milk for coffee.** Oatside oat milk (made in Thailand) shows 13,230 litres, 36% of plant-milk litres; imported oat and almond milks together are about 40%. Oat milk costs VND 75,000 to 117,000 per 20 g of protein against about VND 22,700 for dairy milk, so it is bought for foam and taste, not protein (our calculation) [VN-direct, Low, ECR-06; FORM-01; DIE-12, revealed].
7. **No sign that a protein claim sells in the kitchen channel.** GoldSoy *giàu đạm* (high in protein) soy milk shows 2,609 units at VND 25,000 a litre, against 5,758 for Vinamilk unsweetened soy milk (VND 29,160) and 5,828 for SuperSoy unsweetened (VND 32,000) [VN-direct, Low, ECR-06, revealed].
8. **Imported analogues barely move, and neither do most Vietnamese processed meats.** The eight Meat Zero SKUs show 386 units (80 kg) and the Vissan chay spring roll 0; Panko dry chay pieces 177 units (240 kg). Sausages show 19,161 units (9,395 kg), 117 times Meat Zero by weight, but *giò lụa* (steamed pork loaf) and *chả* (meat loaf, often fried) show 7 units in all [VN-direct, Low, ECR-06; CPC-12, revealed]. Kitchens probably buy *giò* from local makers, so this platform cannot test chay against *giò* (our inference).
9. **Tofu is the plant protein kitchens buy at scale.** 32 soy tofu SKUs show 395,624 units (80,800 kg); the three best sellers alone (76,971 kg) outweigh CP's fresh chicken breast (70,297 kg), though they carry about 5.3 t of protein against 14.3 t (our calculation) [VN-direct, Low, ECR-06; CPC-12; FORM-01; DIE-12, revealed].
10. **At a mother-and-baby chain, nut milk sells beside dairy.** On AVAKids, Vinamilk's 9-nut milk (reduced sugar) shows 9,500 sold, second among the 15 milk product groups read after TH true MILK 110 ml (10,900); its unsweetened twin shows 693 [VN-direct, Low, ECR-07, revealed].
11. **Plant protein powder is a small, dearer niche at a gym-supplement chain.** WheyShop lists 2 plant powders among 25 protein powders (1.7% of their combined counters); per 20 g of protein, whey costs VND 20,800 to 35,000 and plant powders VND 36,000 to 54,800. The package's premium-store median (VND 71,900) overstates what gym users pay (our calculation) [VN-direct, Low, ECR-08; FORM-01, revealed].
12. **Some shop counters are not real counts.** On WheyShop, 24 counter values are shared by 55 of 144 products (for example 2,390 on three different whey powders and 1,062 on four unrelated products), which real counts would rarely produce [VN-direct, Medium, ECR-08, inferred]. Counters on brands' own shop platforms need checking before use.

## 1. Method and reach

We read the ch23 consumer chapter, Appendix AI, the Part VI data files named in the brief, the incumbents and investors actor papers (AIB-05, AIS-25, AIS-26) and wave 7 line L4, which had already read Kamereo's Meat Zero, tofu and meat pages (CPC-12, CPC-13) and Co.op and WinMart listings (CPC-15, CPC-19). We did not repeat that work; we reuse its figures by ID. No search engine was used. We reached pages by known addresses, sitemaps, category links and the sites' own listing pages, respected robots.txt (including Kamereo's 10-second crawl delay) and did not work round any block.

From Kamereo's sitemap we selected every product URL for soy, nut and oat milk, fresh dairy milk, tofu, Meat Zero, Panko, Vissan chay, *giò*, *chả* (meat loaf, often fried), sausages and pate: 143 pages, of which 142 returned data and 140 were in scope. We add CPC-12's five meat and egg comparators, read the same day. `data_sku_counters.csv` holds 185 rows (145 Kamereo, 15 AVAKids, 25 WheyShop); `data_sellthrough_summary.csv` sums them by group; `data_platform_access.csv` and `data_published_reports.csv` record access and the published reports.

**Target versus result.** The brief asked for 40 to 100 SKUs from consumer marketplaces and store sites. We have 185 SKUs with counters, but only 40 come from consumer-facing shops (AVAKids and WheyShop); the rest are kitchen purchases. No chay food, tofu, *giò* or sausage counter from a consumer channel was reachable.

### 3.3 Consumers: milk at a mother-and-baby chain (AVAKids)

AVAKids shows one counter per product group, shared by the 4-pack and the case and rounded above 1,000, so the unit is undefined and litres cannot be computed. On page 1 of fresh milk (13 groups) and the whole nut-milk category (2 groups), counters are: TH true MILK unsweetened 110 ml 10,900; Vinamilk 9-nut reduced sugar 9,500; Dalat Milk 220 ml bag 8,600; Dutch Lady reduced sugar 180 ml 2,600; Dalat Milk 180 ml 2,600; Dutch Lady *cao khỏe* (growth) 110 ml 1,400; the rest 64 to 1,000; Vinamilk 9-nut unsweetened 693 [VN-direct, Low, ECR-07, revealed]. Nut milk is about a quarter of the counters read, but page 2 of fresh milk was not read and the chain's shoppers are parents of young children. The consumer preference runs to sweetened products, the reverse of the kitchen channel.

### 3.6 Do protein-claimed products sell more?

Only two within-category tests are possible, and both are weak.

- **Soy milk for kitchens.** GoldSoy *giàu đạm* unsweetened (2,609) outsold GoldSoy sweetened (880) at the same price, but unsweetened versions also outsell sweeter ones without a claim (1.5 to 1.9 times for SuperSoy and Vinamilk). Against other unsweetened soy milks, the protein-claimed one sold less than half, despite the lowest price; it is out of stock, as is SuperSoy [VN-direct, Low, ECR-06, revealed].
- **Protein powders.** Every product is sold on protein, so the question is plant against whey: plant powders are 2 of 25 and rank below the whey median [VN-direct, Low, ECR-08, revealed].

Vinamilk's *Sữa Hạt Cao Đạm* (high-protein nut milk), the product ch23 relies on, did not appear on any counter we could read.

## 4. Does this change Part VI?

- **ch23 consumer picture.** No. The counters repeat what the shelf audit, search data and firm totals say: plant milk sells (and, in cafés, oat milk sells for coffee), tofu is the plant protein bought in bulk, and imported analogues do not move. One qualification: the "protein now sells" signal in 23.5 is not visible in kitchen purchases.
- **TPP-02 (higher-protein chay foods).** No change. No consumer counter for chay foods was reachable; in the kitchen channel frozen chay and *giò* both barely sell, so the platform cannot test the profile.
- **TPP-05 and T10 (non-soy isolate for plant milks).** No change to the profile or the score. A second protein-claimed plant milk (GoldSoy) exists, a possible buyer to note, and its weak sales are a caution against pricing an isolate on the assumption that a protein claim earns a premium. The imported barista oat milk bought by cafés is a use T10 does not yet consider; it is a question for a later line, not evidence for a change.
- **ch25 channels.** Moderate update: add Metric's 2026 figures and reframe e-commerce as an incumbents' channel.

## Next-wave candidates

1. **Marketplace SKU counters through a browser-based collector.** A *deeper* version of this question. Not closable with the desk tools available (it needs a browser tool or a person). It would change ch23 only if analogue or high-protein SKUs showed sustained sales, which the shelf audit, search data and these counters make unlikely. We think further desk work on this line is noise.
2. **One paid Metric.vn category report** for *sữa hạt* (nut or seed milk), *sữa đậu nành* (soy milk), *sữa yến mạch* (oat milk), *đồ chay* (chay foods) and plant protein. *Deeper*. Desk-closable only with a small budget or the 7-day trial. It closes OQ-187 and OQ-223 and would change ch25 or ch28 only if plant milk online is a large or fast-growing share of liquid milk.
3. **A protein-claim test.** A *different kind* of question (tested, not revealed): a paired listing on Shopee or in 20 minimarts, same product with and without a numeric claim. Not desk work. A clear lift would strengthen TPP-02 and TPP-05; no lift would move their pitch from claims to price.
4. **Kitchen demand data from a distributor.** *Different kind*: ask Kamereo (and MM Mega Market) for 12-month sales by SKU. Not desk work, but one email. It would test the canteen and restaurant rung of the demand model with real volumes of tofu, dry chay pieces and meat.
5. **Café barista plant milk.** *Different kind*: annual volumes and specifications from the Oatside distributor and two café chains. Partly desk work. If volumes are material, a local rice, mung bean or soy barista base becomes a nearer use for T10 than UHT consumer milk.

## Limits

- The major marketplaces, where consumer counters live, were not readable; our consumer evidence is two categories at one mother-and-baby chain and one gym-supplement shop.
- All counters are cumulative with unknown start dates, and stock-outs and listing age bias the comparisons. Shares are orders of magnitude, not market shares.
- Kamereo serves restaurants, cafés and canteens in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi, not households. SKUs were chosen by URL keywords from its sitemap, which may miss items; one page returned no data.
- AVAKids was read through the fetch tool's summary, which we could not check against raw HTML; only page 1 of fresh milk was read.
- WheyShop's counters are shop-set and repeat across products.
- Protein values are borrowed from other SKUs' labels or the food composition table and are given as ranges.
- Metric does not publish its method or category definitions; its figures are platform-data estimates.
- A single snapshot on 25 September 2026.
