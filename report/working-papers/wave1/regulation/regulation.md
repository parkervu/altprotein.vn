# M5 Rules of the game: legal routes, incentives and reforms for new proteins in Vietnam (as of 23 September 2026)

Agent: REGULATION (wave 1). Source prefix: REG. Evidence labels: [VN-direct], [VN-adjacent], [general]. Confidence: High = primary legal text read by this agent (through a text-extraction tool, see method note); Medium = reputable secondary (government press, law-firm, USDA, trade press); Low = single claim, or background knowledge not re-verified in this wave.

Method note. Legal texts were read on thuvienphapluat.vn, luatvietnam.vn and government portals through a fetch tool that returns extracts and paraphrases, not full verbatim text. Where this note says "High", the wording was extracted from the primary text itself. The web-search budget ran out early in this wave, so several threads (ASEAN, Codex, foreign precedents, some Vietnamese appendices) could not be verified and are flagged. The MOH portal and fas.usda.gov were unreachable (timeouts, HTTP 403).

Terms used:
- Self-declaration (tự công bố sản phẩm): the business files its own product specification and a lab test report and may sell at once. No agency approval.
- Registration of product declaration (đăng ký bản công bố sản phẩm): the agency reviews the dossier and issues a receipt before sale.
- Feed trial (khảo nghiệm): a controlled animal feeding trial under an approved protocol, whose recognised result is needed before an unlisted feed ingredient can be used.
- Permitted list (danh mục nguyên liệu được phép sử dụng): the Ministry list of raw materials that may be used in feed without a trial.
- MAE: Ministry of Agriculture and Environment (Bộ Nông nghiệp và Môi trường), formed 1 March 2025 from MARD and MONRE.
- MOH/VFA: Ministry of Health / Vietnam Food Administration (Cục An toàn thực phẩm).
- DLPAH: Department of Livestock Production and Animal Health (Cục Chăn nuôi và Thú y), the post-2025 successor of the Department of Livestock Production.
- DFFI: Department of Fisheries and Fisheries Surveillance (Cục Thủy sản và Kiểm ngư).

## Headline findings

1. **Food law is in flux, and the 2018 rules still apply.** Market entry for food products still runs on Decree 15/2018/ND-CP (self-declaration, Art 4 to 5; registration, Art 6 to 8). Its intended replacement, Decree 46/2026/ND-CP of 26 January 2026, and Resolution 66.13/2026/NQ-CP of 27 January 2026 caused port congestion. The Government suspended both (Resolution 09/2026/NQ-CP, 4 February 2026, then Resolution 15/2026/NQ-CP, 6 April 2026). The suspension lasts until the revised Food Safety Law and its implementing decree take effect. [VN-direct] High. REG-02, REG-05, REG-06, REG-08.
2. **A replacement Food Safety Law is before the National Assembly, and we found no novel-food category in it.** The Ministry of Health (MOH) drafted it: 8 chapters, 71 articles, notified to the WTO as G/SPS/N/VNM/187 on 3 August 2026. It is due for review at the National Assembly's second 2026 session (October 2026). One trade source gives a target entry into force of 1 January 2027. None of the draft summaries, the MOH submission or the stakeholder comments we read mentions "thực phẩm mới" (novel food), new ingredients or a sandbox. We did not read the full draft text. [VN-direct] Medium. REG-10, REG-12, REG-13, REG-14, REG-16.
3. **Vietnam has no novel-food gate, but the law has a latent hook against "new-technology" food.** No law or decree defines novel food, history of safe use, or a pre-market safety assessment for new food sources. A new protein food therefore enters, in practice, through self-declaration against generic safety limits. But the Food Safety Law bans "using raw materials not of the type used for food" (Art 5(1)). Art 55(1)(c) also lists "food that is a new-technology product not yet permitted for circulation" among foods subject to recall, and no procedure exists to obtain that permission. The result is legal uncertainty, not a clear route. [VN-direct] High (text); Medium (interpretation). REG-01, REG-02.
4. **The GMO rules were overhauled in January 2026.** Decree 43/2026/ND-CP (effective 26 January 2026) amended Decree 69/2010. It has four main effects:
   - One MAE procedure now certifies GMOs for both food and feed use. The safety council has 67 working days for a full review, or 17 working days if at least 5 developed OECD/G20 countries have approved the event.
   - Gene-edited organisms without foreign DNA are handled by self-determination and notification to MAE.
   - A new Art 37a creates a certificate from the MAE Minister for GMOs used in closed ("khép kín") production.
   - "Product of a GMO" now means a product that contains GM genetic material "or protein from the modified gene".
   [VN-direct] High on structure; Medium on timelines. REG-18, REG-19, REG-21, REG-23.
5. **A precision-fermented protein is probably a "product of a GMO" in Vietnam, even when no DNA remains.** A recombinant protein is, literally, protein from a modified gene. So it probably falls in the new definition, and GM labelling applies when a GM ingredient is more than 5% of the product (Decree 15/2018 Art 10). Making it in Vietnam with a GM microbe needs the new closed-production certificate. No text says whether the purified protein also needs a GMO food-use certificate. This is the single most important open question for precision fermentation. [VN-direct] Medium. REG-02, REG-18, REG-19.
6. **Correction to a prior lead: Art 11 of Decree 13/2020 is about factory inspections, not feed trials.** Feed trials come from Art 37 of the Law on Animal Husbandry 2018: feed with a "new substance" not yet trialled in Vietnam, and some first imports. The procedure is in Chapter III (Art 13 to 14) of Circular 94/2025/TT-BNNMT, effective 1 January 2026:
   - DLPAH approves the trial protocol in 15 + 5 working days.
   - It recognises the result in 15 + 5 working days.
   - The length of the trial itself is set case by case.
   - A recognised result is "the basis for adding [the material] to the permitted raw-material list", and that list is updated "periodically every year".
   No text gives the prior lead's "12 to 18 months". [VN-direct] High. REG-29, REG-30, REG-34, REG-32.
7. **Several candidate proteins are already legal livestock-feed raw materials, with no trial needed.** The permitted list (Circular 21/2019/TT-BNNPTNT Appendix VI, amended by Circular 31/2022) already names:
   - insects ("côn trùng") and other invertebrates;
   - aquatic plants including "bèo" (duckweed) and algae ("tảo", "rong");
   - inactive yeasts, since February 2023: Torula or Candida yeast, yeast grown on molasses, and by-products of amino-acid production.
   These still need a certified factory, a conformity declaration against QCVN 01-190:2020/BNNPTNT, and labelling. We found no explicit entry for bacterial single-cell protein, fungal mycoprotein or precision-fermented proteins. [VN-direct] High (listed items); Medium (absence). REG-32, REG-33, REG-36.
8. **Aquafeed has its own list and its own trial route.** Aquafeed containing ingredients not on the MAE permitted list must be trialled (Law on Fisheries Art 31 and 35; Decree 26/2019 Art 31 to 32). The authority appraises the trial dossier in 20 days and recognises results within 10 working days. The permitted list now sits in Appendix II of Circular 16/2026/TT-BNNMT (9 March 2026). We could not read that appendix's raw-material section, so we do not know whether insect meal, yeast or single-cell protein are listed for aquafeed. [VN-direct] High on procedure; gap on list. REG-37, REG-38, REG-40.
9. **Provinces now run most feed procedures, so the choice of province changes time to market.** Since 1 July 2025 (Decree 136/2025) and 21 January 2026 (Decree 32/2026), provincial People's Committee chairs handle:
   - feed factory certificates;
   - review of supplement-feed information before publication (15 working days);
   - import licences for feed not yet published (10 working days);
   - state quality inspection of imported feed;
   - recognition of aquafeed trial results and import licences for unlisted aquafeed.
   [VN-direct] High. REG-41, REG-35.
10. **Tax: new corporate income tax (CIT) incentives favour high-tech biotech, but the project must qualify.** The Law on Corporate Income Tax 67/2025/QH15 took effect on 1 October 2025 and applies from the 2025 tax year. It gives a 10% rate for 15 years, plus a 4-year exemption and a 50% cut for 9 years, to high-tech enterprises, high-tech agriculture enterprises and new projects applying listed high technology. Agricultural and aquatic processing gets preferential rates (10% or 15%, depending on location). [VN-direct] Medium to High. REG-45.
11. **Precision fermentation is on the new priority high-tech list; "alternative protein" as such is not.** Decision 23/2026/QĐ-TTg (effective 1 July 2026, replacing Decision 38/2020) lists:
   - synthetic biology (item 49), new-generation microbial technology (item 51), and tissue and cell technology in agriculture (item 52);
   - "technology for producing recombinant proteins, enzymes and monoclonal antibodies" (item 60);
   - among encouraged products: recombinant proteins and enzymes (item 92), and microbial strains and products for agriculture (item 100).
   There is no item for food, feed or alternative protein as such. [VN-direct] High. REG-47.
12. **The Investment Support Fund is out of reach for alt protein.** Prior thresholds in Decree 182/2024/ND-CP are confirmed: VND 12,000 bn capital or VND 20,000 bn annual revenue for high-tech product projects (VND 6,000 bn or 10,000 bn for semiconductors and AI), and VND 3,000 bn for R&D centres, of which VND 1,000 bn within 3 years. No alt-protein project in Vietnam is near these sizes. [VN-direct] High. REG-46.
13. **A general regulatory sandbox now exists in law, but none is built for food.** The Law on Science, Technology and Innovation 93/2025/QH15 (effective 1 October 2025), Art 21 to 23, allows controlled testing of technologies, products and business models "not yet regulated or differing from current law". The test lasts up to 3 years, extendable once by 3 years, with civil, administrative and criminal liability exemptions. Resolution 193/2025/QH15's sandbox covers only low-earth-orbit satellites. No food or feed sandbox exists, and we did not find an implementing decree naming the approving body for food. [VN-direct] High on law text; gap on implementation. REG-48, REG-49.
14. **Strategies back biotech in general but never name alternative protein.** Resolution 36-NQ/TW (2023) targets a biotech industry worth 7% of GDP by 2030. The livestock strategy (Decision 1520/QĐ-TTg, 2020) targets 30 to 32 Mt of industrial feed output by 2030 and promotes by-products (brewery waste, cassava residue) and biotech in feed. The food-systems plan (Decision 300/QĐ-TTg, 2023) targets a 10% cut in food-system GHG emissions against 2020. None of them names alternative, novel or microbial protein. [VN-direct] Medium to High. REG-51, REG-52, REG-54, REG-55.
15. **Halal: a voluntary national framework has been in force since 1 June 2026.** Decree 127/2026/ND-CP sets voluntary halal certification. Certifiers register with MOST and need at least 2 Muslim expert assessors. The state budget can support testing and certification costs for export-oriented firms. The decree is silent on biotech, GM-derived or fermentation-derived ingredients. [VN-direct] High. REG-27.
16. **Commercialising Vietnamese microbial strains needs an access licence and benefit sharing.** Decree 59/2017/ND-CP is still in force and does not exclude microorganisms. Commercial use of Vietnamese genetic resources needs a licence: a council review of up to 90 days, then a decision within 15 days. Benefit sharing is at least 1% of annual product revenue, or at least 2% of the value of an IP transfer. Law 146/2025/QH15 restructured the access-permit article of the Biodiversity Law from 1 January 2026. [VN-direct] High on Decree 59; Medium on Law 146 details. REG-60, REG-43, REG-61.
17. **Who owns what after the 2025 restructuring.**
   - MAE (from 1 March 2025, Decree 35/2025) holds feed (DLPAH), aquafeed and fisheries (DFFI), GMO biosafety and biodiversity (Department of Nature Conservation and Biodiversity), the carbon market (Department of Climate Change), and primary agri-food quality (Department of Quality, Processing and Market Development).
   - MOH/VFA holds processed food declarations, additives and health supplements.
   - The draft Food Safety Law would make MOH the single focal point for food safety.
   [VN-direct] High. REG-42, REG-14, REG-15.
18. **Singapore contrast.** Since 28 November 2025, supplying an unapproved novel food (no 20-year history of use, including cultivated and precision-fermented products) is illegal in Singapore under the Food Safety and Security Act. Vietnam has no equivalent. A Singapore approval gives no legal status in Vietnam, and Vietnam's lack of a gate gives no certainty to regional buyers. [VN-adjacent] High. REG-63.

## Detailed findings

### A. Food

#### A1. Law on Food Safety 55/2010/QH12 and the revision
- **Status.** Law 55/2010/QH12 is in force. It was amended in 2018 by Law 28/2018/QH14, and a consolidated text (61/VBHN-VPQH) was issued in 2026. [VN-direct] High. REG-01, REG-17.
- **Relevant text:**
  - Art 2 defines food broadly: products people eat or drink, fresh or processed.
  - Art 5(1) prohibits "Sử dụng nguyên liệu không thuộc loại dùng cho thực phẩm để chế biến thực phẩm" (using raw materials not of the type used for food to process food).
  - Art 5(5)(h) prohibits selling food not registered when registration is required.
  - Art 15 covers GM food (safety conditions and labelling "thực phẩm biến đổi gen").
  - Art 55(1)(c) lists "Thực phẩm là sản phẩm công nghệ mới chưa được phép lưu hành" (food that is a new-technology product not yet permitted for circulation) among foods to be recalled.
  - No implementing text defines "new-technology product" or says how permission is obtained. [VN-direct] High. REG-01.
- **Revision timeline:**

| Date | Step | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 24 Sep 2025 | Deputy PM asks to "decode" the single-focal-point question; MOH proposes itself as the single agency; law to go to the National Assembly in 2026 | REG-15 (MAE portal) |
| 16 Jan 2026 | Resolution 14/NQ-CP proposes adding the revised Food Safety Law to the 2026 legislative programme; MOH leads | REG-09 |
| 26 to 31 Mar 2026 | Draft framework published; 4-day comment window | REG-08 |
| 23 and 31 Jul 2026 | Draft at 8 chapters and 71 articles after 22 drafting sessions. Four policy groups: supply-chain risk management, a shift from pre-market to post-market control, digital systems, street food and catering | REG-10, REG-11 |
| 3 Aug 2026 | WTO notification G/SPS/N/VNM/187; comments due 17 Aug 2026; "legislative approval target October 2026; entry into force 1 January 2027" | REG-12 (trade secondary) |
| 4 Sep 2026 | EuroCham warns that the proposed product declaration changes would raise administrative costs; asks to keep the Decree 15/2018 reforms | REG-13 |
| 22 Sep 2026 | A National Assembly deputy on the Science, Technology and Environment Committee says the law will be reviewed at the second 2026 session. He says the implementing decree makes MOH the main authority, and that "product registration procedures risk becoming pre-approval mechanisms" | REG-14 |

- **Novel food concept.** None of REG-08 to REG-16 mentions a novel food, new ingredient, history-of-use or sandbox concept. The July 2025 MOH submission (REG-16) proposed three risk tiers for product management (low: self-declare; medium: self-declare plus conformity assessment; high: third-party certification), aligned with the 2025 revision of the Law on Product and Goods Quality. It also contained nothing on novel foods. [VN-direct] Medium, because the full draft text was not read (MOH portal unreachable).

#### A2. Decree 15/2018/ND-CP and its suspended replacement
- **Status (23 Sep 2026): applies.** Decree 46/2026/ND-CP (26 Jan 2026) was designed to replace it. Resolution 09/2026/NQ-CP (4 Feb 2026) suspended Decree 46 until 15 Apr 2026 and restored Decree 15/2018. Resolution 15/2026/NQ-CP (6 Apr 2026) suspended Decree 46/2026 and Resolution 66.13/2026 "until the amended Food Safety Law and its guiding decree take effect", and Decree 15/2018 "continues to have effect". [VN-direct] High (government press). REG-05, REG-06.
- **Self-declaration (Art 4 to 5):**
  - Covers processed pre-packaged food, food additives, processing aids, and packaging in direct contact with food.
  - Exempt (Art 4(2)): products and raw materials made or imported only for export processing, or "phục vụ cho việc sản xuất nội bộ" (for the firm's own internal production) and not sold domestically. This matters for imported alt-protein ingredients used in-house.
  - The dossier is a self-declaration form plus a test report from a designated or accredited lab, dated within 12 months.
  - The business may produce and sell right after filing with the authority designated by the provincial People's Committee (or publishing).
  [VN-direct] High. REG-02.
- **Registration (Art 6 to 8):**
  - Required for health supplements (thực phẩm bảo vệ sức khỏe), medical foods, special-diet foods, products for children up to 36 months, and "mixed additives with new uses or additives not on the MOH permitted list or not used as permitted".
  - Health supplements are registered at MOH within 21 working days; the other categories take 7 working days.
  - The authority may ask for corrections once; the applicant then has 90 working days.
  [VN-direct] High. REG-02.
- **No provision covers foods with no history of use or new ingredients.** GM food: Art 9 refers to Decree 69/2010 for the food-use certificate. Art 10 requires GM labelling when at least one GM ingredient is more than 5% of total ingredients. [VN-direct] High. REG-02.
- **What Decree 46/2026 would have changed (now suspended):**
  - Registration of conformity declarations for pre-packaged processed foods, additives, processing aids and packaging.
  - Declarations valid for up to 3 years.
  - Mandatory food safety management systems (HACCP or similar) by 31 Dec 2026.
  - Tighter import inspection, and joint liability for e-commerce platforms.
  - Nothing on novel foods.
  - Resolution 66.13/2026 would have replaced self-declaration with registration for several categories, with up to 90 days processing and a 24-month transition.
  These signal the direction MOH may take in the new law: less pure self-declaration. [VN-direct] Medium. REG-03, REG-04, REG-07.

#### A3. GMO food and precision fermentation
- **Instruments:** Decree 69/2010/ND-CP, amended by Decree 108/2011, Decree 118/2020 and now Decree 43/2026/ND-CP (26 Jan 2026, effective the same day). Decree 43/2026 amends Decree 65/2010 (Biodiversity Law) and Decree 69/2010 (biosafety). It does not amend Decree 59/2017. [VN-direct] High. REG-18, REG-24.
- **Changes:**
  - (a) Scope now includes gene-edited organisms (sinh vật chỉnh sửa gen). The Biodiversity Law, as amended by Law 146/2025, defines these as containing no new genetic material. Owners self-determine and report to MAE, and keep records for 10 years.
  - (b) "Product of a GMO" means "sản phẩm được tạo ra từ sinh vật biến đổi gen có chứa vật liệu di truyền hoặc protein từ gen biến đổi" (a product created from a GMO that contains GM genetic material or protein from the modified gene).
  - (c) Art 32 to 33 are merged into one MAE procedure certifying a GMO as eligible for use as food and feed. Criteria: a safety council finds no uncontrollable risk, or at least 5 developed OECD/G20 countries allow it. Steps: 5 working days to accept and form the council; 67 working days (full review) or 17 working days (5-country route) for the council; 5 working days to decide. MOH is consulted during a 20-day public comment period. A GMO approved as food qualifies as feed.
  - (d) New Art 37a: "Giấy chứng nhận sinh vật biến đổi gen đủ điều kiện sử dụng trong sản xuất khép kín" (certificate that a GMO is eligible for use in closed production), issued by the MAE Minister. The system must prevent release, have approved waste treatment, and meet OECD/G20-type standards. We could not read its timeline.
  [VN-direct] High on (a), (b), (d) wording; Medium on timelines. REG-18, REG-21, REG-22, REG-23, REG-19.
- **Refined products.** USDA (REG-19) and ISAAA (REG-20) report that highly refined products such as soybean oil and corn starch are excluded from "GM food". The text extract did not show an explicit exclusion, but definition (b) already excludes anything without GM DNA or GM protein. [VN-adjacent reading of VN-direct text] Medium.
- **Implications for precision fermentation (PF):**
  - A purified recombinant protein contains "protein from the modified gene", so it is likely a "product of a GMO".
  - Labelling as GM applies above 5%.
  - The production strain needs the closed-production certificate (Art 37a) if used in Vietnam.
  - The text is silent on whether the protein also needs the food-use certificate, which is written for organisms, not products. Imported PF ingredients face the same labelling question.
  [VN-direct] Medium (interpretation).
- **Authority change:** food-use certification of GMOs is now fully with MAE, with MOH consulted. [VN-direct] Medium to High. REG-21, REG-22.

#### A4. Additives, enzymes, processing aids, health supplements
- **Circular 24/2019/TT-BYT** (effective 16 Oct 2019) sets the permitted additive list. Art 4 bases it on CAC and JECFA. New uses, or mixed additives with new functions, must be registered at VFA (Art 11(2)). Organisations may propose additions with scientific safety evidence (Art 17(3)(d)). The extract did not show a separate pathway for enzymes or processing aids, and nothing on GM-derived additives. Amendments after 2019 were not visible. [VN-direct] Medium to High. REG-25.
- **Route for a new additive or enzyme:** registration of the product declaration at VFA (Decree 15/2018 Art 6, 7 working days), or a request to MOH to amend the list. [VN-direct] Medium.
- **Health supplements:** MOH registration (21 working days) and a GMP-certified plant (Decree 15/2018 Art 7, 11, 12). This is a real route for microalgae (spirulina, chlorella) and concentrated protein ingredients sold as supplements. [VN-direct] High. REG-02.

#### A5. Labelling
- Decree 43/2017/ND-CP as amended by Decree 111/2021/ND-CP (effective 15 Feb 2022) requires for food: name, ingredients or quantified ingredients, nutrition (if any), "thông tin cảnh báo" (warnings), dates, storage and use. [VN-direct] High. REG-26.
- **Meat and milk names:** no provision found that restricts terms such as "thịt" (meat) or "sữa" (milk) for plant-based products, or that defines "chay" (vegetarian). Market practice (sữa đậu nành, sữa hạt, giò chay) relies on general rules against misleading labels. [VN-direct] Medium (absence in the texts read). REG-26.
- **Allergens:** allergen-specific rules were not visible in the extract. We believe the warning requirement covers allergens (guidance under Circular 05/2019/TT-BKHCN), but this was not verified in this wave. Low.
- **GM labelling:** above 5% GM ingredient (Decree 15/2018 Art 10). Nutrition labelling: Circular 29/2023/TT-BYT (listed, not read). REG-02, REG-17.

#### A6. Halal
- Decision 10/QĐ-TTg (2024) approves the scheme to strengthen international cooperation for Vietnam's halal industry to 2030 (listed, not read). [VN-direct] Low to Medium. REG-28.
- Decree 127/2026/ND-CP (6 Apr 2026, effective 1 Jun 2026) is the first decree on halal quality management and development:
  - Certification is voluntary unless another law requires it.
  - Certifiers register with MOST (Art 13) and need at least 2 Muslim expert assessors (Art 11).
  - Standards may be TCVN (e.g. TCVN 13888:2023), international or partner-country standards.
  - Budget support for export-oriented testing and certification (Art 23), and mutual recognition (Art 16).
  - Nothing on GM, fermentation or cell-based ingredients. The name of the national halal centre (HALCERT) did not appear in the extract.
  [VN-direct] High. REG-27.
- **Relevance:** halal certification of fermentation-derived proteins (media, strains) will follow the certifier's standard. Export to Malaysia or Indonesia needs recognition by JAKIM or BPJPH, which this wave did not verify.

#### A7. Import of food ingredients
- Under Decree 15/2018 (applicable), imported food is subject to state inspection, with exemptions (Art 13): self-declared products for own use, personal and diplomatic imports, goods for export processing, and raw materials for internal production. [VN-direct] Medium to High. REG-02.
- Decree 46/2026 would have tightened import inspection (it caused port congestion in Jan to Feb 2026) but is suspended. MOIT issued Circular 28/2026/TT-BCT on the list of imported goods under food safety inspection (listed, not read). [VN-direct] Medium. REG-08, REG-17.

### B. Feed

#### B1. Livestock feed: how a new single raw material becomes legal

**Legal chain:**
- Law on Animal Husbandry 32/2018/QH14, amended by Law 146/2025/QH15 from 1 Jan 2026.
  - Art 32: before circulation, commercial feed needs a published standard and conformity declaration, a certified factory, product information on the MAE portal, and a label.
  - Art 33: compound and concentrate feed are self-published.
  - Art 34: supplement feed is appraised before publication.
  - Art 36: MAE publishes traditional feeds and single raw materials by name and technical requirements. Feed for internal use and custom orders is exempt.
  - Art 37: feed trials. Required for (a) certain first imports from countries whose trial procedures Vietnam does not recognise, and (b) feed containing a "chất mới" (new substance) not yet trialled in Vietnam, except results of recognised state research projects. Art 37(4) empowers the Minister to issue the permitted raw-material list.
  [VN-direct] High on structure (paraphrased extraction). REG-29, REG-43.
- Decree 13/2020/ND-CP, amended by Decree 46/2022 and Decree 32/2026:
  - Art 9 to 11: factory conditions and certificates. Art 11 is on-site assessment of factory conditions, with surveillance every 24 months (36 months with ISO, GMP or HACCP).
  - Art 13: import of feed not yet published (licensing).
  - Art 15: recognition of exporting countries' trial and recognition procedures.
  - Art 17 to 18: state quality inspection.
  - None of these articles is about trials of new feed. Art 31 is about trials of livestock waste-treatment products.
  [VN-direct] High. REG-30.
- Circular 21/2019/TT-BNNPTNT, amended by Circular 31/2022/TT-BNNPTNT:
  - Art 6: the permitted list is Appendix VI. DLPAH "tổng hợp, trình Bộ trưởng … cập nhật Danh mục … định kỳ hằng năm" (compiles and submits to the Minister to update the list periodically every year).
  - Appendix VI, section I.1.2, terrestrial animal origin: "bột xương, bột thịt, … côn trùng, động vật không xương sống …" (bone meal, meat meal, … insects, invertebrates …).
  - Section I.2, plant origin: coarse feeds include aquatic plants "rong, rêu, tảo, bèo" (seaweed, moss, algae, duckweed).
  - Section II: vitamins, minerals, amino acids, enzymes, probiotics (Bacillus, Lactobacillus, Saccharomyces cerevisiae) and technical additives.
  - Circular 31/2022 (effective 20 Feb 2023) added inactive yeasts to I.6: irradiated, hydrolysed, dried Torula or Candida yeast, yeast cultured on molasses, dried yeast, and by-products from amino-acid production.
  [VN-direct] High. REG-32, REG-33.
- Circular 94/2025/TT-BNNMT (effective 1 Jan 2026), Chapter III (Art 13 to 14), feed trials:
  - Trial content: quality, safety or toxicity, and effects on animals. Methods suit each feed type, and duration is set in the protocol.
  - DLPAH receives the protocol, forms an appraisal council within 15 working days, and approves within 5 working days after revision.
  - Results recognition takes 15 + 5 working days.
  - "Kết quả khảo nghiệm được công nhận là căn cứ để đưa vào danh mục nguyên liệu được phép sử dụng làm thức ăn chăn nuôi" (a recognised trial result is the basis for adding the material to the permitted list).
  - It replaced parts of Circular 22/2019, Circular 21/2024 and Circular 09/2025. It did not visibly replace Circular 21/2019.
  [VN-direct] High. REG-34.
- QCVN 01-190:2020/BNNPTNT (Circular 04/2020, amended by Circulars 08/2020 and 05/2021) sets maximum limits for arsenic, cadmium, lead, mercury, aflatoxin B1, E. coli, Salmonella, HCN, gossypol and others in feed and raw materials. **So, for a NEW single raw material (e.g. bacterial SCP, mycoprotein, cassava-pulp microbial protein):**
1. Check the permitted list (Circular 21/2019 Appendix VI as amended). If it fits an existing entry (e.g. "nấm men" yeast, "côn trùng" insects, "bèo" duckweed), no trial is needed.
2. If not, it is a "new substance": run a trial under an approved protocol (Circular 94/2025 Ch. III) at a qualified trial facility (Law Art 37(3)). Importing trial samples may need a provincial import licence (Decree 32/2026).
3. Get the result recognised by DLPAH, then wait for the list update (annual) or rely on the recognition decision.
4. Certified factory (provincial), standard and conformity declaration against QCVN 01-190:2020, label.
5. If sold as supplement feed (functional claim), province appraisal (15 working days, valid 5 years).

**Documented agency time:** about 40 working days (protocol 15 + 5; results 15 + 5). This excludes dossier preparation, the trial itself (likely one grow-out cycle per species) and the wait for the annual list update. Our estimate of the total is 6 to 18 months. That is an estimate, not a documented figure. [VN-direct] Medium.

## How a new protein would get to market in Vietnam today (plain-language explainer)

These are the routes as the law stood on 23 September 2026. They may change when the new Food Safety Law takes effect (possibly 1 January 2027).

1. **Plant-based food from known ingredients** (tofu, textured soy, gluten, jackfruit, "đồ chay"). Make it in a food-safe factory. Test the product at an accredited lab. File a self-declaration with the provincial food safety authority. You can sell right away. Label it under the labelling decree. No rule stops you calling it "chay" or using words like "sữa hạt", but do not mislead. Many brands already do this.
2. **Koji or other traditional mould fermentation on a food substrate** (Aspergillus oryzae, Rhizopus). The route is the same as (1). The organisms have long food use in Vietnam (tương, nước tương) and elsewhere, so legal risk is low. Keep strain identity and safety data on file.
3. **Fungal biomass or mycoprotein food.** Legally, the only route is the same self-declaration as (1). There is no way to get the ingredient pre-approved. If the fungus has no food history, an inspector could argue it is "not a food-type raw material" (Law Art 5(1)) or a "new-technology product not yet permitted" (Art 55(1)(c)). Edible-mushroom species carry lower risk than new strains. Build a safety dossier and talk to VFA first.
4. **Microalgae food** (spirulina, chlorella). Either self-declare as an ordinary food, or register as a health supplement at MOH (21 working days, GMP factory). Newer algae species carry the same uncertainty as (3).
5. **Precision-fermented protein food.** An imported ingredient can be self-declared, or is exempt from declaration if used only in your own production. Label it as GM if it is more than 5% of the product, because a recombinant protein is probably a "product of a GMO". Making it in Vietnam with a GM microbe needs the new MAE certificate for GMOs used in closed production. If it works as an additive or enzyme that is not on the MOH list, register it with VFA. No text says whether a GMO food-use certificate is also needed, so ask MAE and MOH in writing before investing.
6. **Cultivated meat or seafood.** No workable route today. There is no procedure to obtain the "permission to circulate" the law implies for new-technology food. Any genetically modified cell line adds GMO questions. The only realistic path is a supervised pilot under the new science law's sandbox (Law 93/2025 Art 21), which needs a willing ministry.
7. **Insect meal for livestock and poultry feed.** Insects are on the permitted raw-material list, so no feeding trial is needed. You need a certified feed factory (province), a declared standard and conformity with QCVN 01-190:2020 limits, and a label. Entobel has done this since 2019. For shrimp and fish feed, first check whether insect meal is on the aquafeed list; if not, run an aquafeed trial.
8. **Duckweed or algae meal for feed.** "Bèo" (duckweed) and algae are on the livestock list, so the route is the same as (7). If grown on wastewater, test heavy metals and pathogens carefully against QCVN 01-190:2020.
9. **Yeast protein for feed.** Inactive yeasts, including Torula or Candida yeast and yeast grown on molasses, and by-products of amino-acid fermentation, have been on the livestock list since February 2023. The route is the same as (7).
10. **Bacterial single-cell protein or other new microbial protein for livestock feed.** This is probably a "new substance". Run a feed trial with a DLPAH-approved protocol (about 20 working days), then get the result recognised (about 20 working days). The material then enters the permitted list at the next annual update. Our total estimate is 6 to 18 months. That is an estimate, not a legal deadline.
11. **Single-cell protein for aquafeed.** If the ingredient is not on the aquafeed list (Circular 16/2026 Appendix II), register a trial (20-day dossier appraisal), run it, and get the results recognised by the provincial People's Committee (10 working days). Importing trial material needs a provincial licence. Then declare the aquafeed products that contain it.

## Implications by audience

- **VCs and investors**
  - The binding legal risk is on the food side, and it is uncertainty rather than prohibition. It can be priced as "enforcement discretion" risk until the new law and decree are out (earliest 1 Jan 2027). Diligence should ask for a VFA or MAE written opinion.
  - Feed plays using insects, yeast or duckweed face no trial barrier. Bacterial SCP or other new microbes need a trial of perhaps 6 to 18 months (estimate).
  - Precision fermentation qualifies as priority high-tech (Decision 23/2026 items 60 and 92). That can unlock 10% CIT for 15 years and 4 + 9 years of holidays, but it also triggers GMO labelling and a closed-production certificate.
- **Policy makers**
  - The October 2026 Food Safety Law session is a live, one-off window to define "new food / new food ingredient" and a proportionate procedure. Without it, Art 55(1)(c) remains a dead-letter hook.
  - MAE can add candidate feed materials (bacterial SCP, fungal biomass) to the permitted lists in the next annual update, using foreign authorisations and state-funded trials, which Art 37(2) exempts.
  - GMO status of purified precision-fermented proteins needs a joint MAE and MOH clarification.
- **Startups**
  - Choose a product whose inputs are already on the lists (feed) or have food history (food).
  - Choose the province with care, because provinces now run feed licences, trial recognition for aquafeed, and import inspection.
  - Budget for Decree 59/2017 benefit sharing if you commercialise a Vietnam-isolated strain.
- **F&B and feed manufacturers**
  - Imported ingredients used only in your own production are exempt from self-declaration (Decree 15/2018 Art 4(2)).
  - Expect declaration rules to tighten under the new law (Decree 46/2026 and Resolution 66.13 show the direction).
  - Feed mills can already use insect meal, duckweed and inactive yeast without trials.
- **Academia**
  - Law Art 37(2) exempts feed from recognised state research projects from separate trials. Public feed trials on SCP in shrimp and pangasius are therefore a high-leverage public good.
- **International bodies**
  - Support for drafting (novel-food guidance, reliance on reference regulators) and for public feed trials fits current windows: the Food Safety Law in 2026 and the MAE annual list updates.
