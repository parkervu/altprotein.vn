# M3 Industrial capacity: fermentation, feed, food and processing capacity in Vietnam that alt-protein ventures can use

Agent: INDUSTRY (wave 1). Source prefix: IND. Edition date: 23 September 2026.
Files: `industry.md` (this note), `facilities.csv` (81 rows), `fermentation_volume_estimate.csv` (modelled tank volumes with assumptions).

Method note. The shared web-search budget for this session ran out after about 20 searches. The rest of the work used direct page fetches of primary and press pages found in those searches, plus the project's own September 2026 retail field log. Several sub-questions (do chay plant capacities, extrusion, protein isolates, spray drying, local fabricators, high-tech agriculture zones, university pilot plants, fishmeal and premix plants) are therefore thin. They are listed under gaps with the cheapest way to close each one.

Terms. "Aerobic, aseptic fermentation" means stirred, aerated, sterilisable tanks, the kind used for amino acids, yeast and precision fermentation. "Anaerobic" tanks (beer, fuel ethanol) are not aerated and usually not sterile. "Nameplate" or "design" capacity is what a plant was built for, not what it makes. "Claimed" means a company or press statement not checked against a filing.

Province names follow the 1 July 2025 merger (63 to 34 units), checked against the Wikipedia list of provinces (IND-66). Format: current province (former province).

## Headline findings

1. **Vietnam's aerobic industrial fermentation base is captive and concentrated in three amino-acid groups.** These are Vedan (Taiwan) and Ajinomoto (Japan), both in Dong Nai, and Daesang/Miwon (Korea) in Phu Tho. Ajinomoto's permitted MSG capacity is 150,000 t/yr (2023 environmental impact assessment (EIA) consultation, reported in press). Vedan says each of its fermentation tanks holds 700 m3. It does not publish its tank count or current MSG capacity. [VN-direct] Medium. IND-01, IND-08, IND-11.
2. **Our modelled estimate of installed aerobic, aseptic fermentation volume is about 11,000 to 34,000 m3 gross.** Almost all of it sits inside those three MSG and lysine producers. Baker's yeast adds under 1,000 m3. Anaerobic volume is much larger: fuel ethanol about 38,000 to 58,000 m3 and brewing about 170,000 to 410,000 m3. That gives a total of roughly 220,000 to 500,000 m3. This is a modelled estimate from published capacities and standard process assumptions, not a measured figure. [VN-direct inputs, general assumptions] Low. `fermentation_volume_estimate.csv`.
3. **We found no Vietnamese company that offers food-grade contract fermentation (CDMO) at pilot or commercial scale.** The large fermenters run single-product, integrated lines. None of their websites or press describes toll fermentation. This is absence of evidence from a limited search. It should be tested by direct calls. [VN-direct] Low. IND-01, IND-02, IND-08, IND-60.
4. **Yeast capacity, the closest process match to yeast or fungal single-cell protein, is small.** AB Mauri La Nga (Dong Nai) reported 6,000 t/yr in 2019, the year it was suspended over pollution. Saf-Viet (Lesaffre) runs a plant in Tay Ninh (former Long An) of undisclosed size. [VN-direct] Medium. IND-15, IND-16, IND-17.
5. **Fuel ethanol is the largest stranded fermentation asset, but the E10 mandate is now pulling it back to ethanol.** Six or seven plants have a design capacity of about 450,000 to 490,000 m3/yr. In mid-2026 only three ran, at about 22,000 to 25,000 m3/month, against E10 demand of 92,000 to 100,000 m3/month. In July 2026, 81,500 m3 of 103,700 m3 supplied was imported. Binh Phuoc (100 million L/yr) has been idle for more than 10 years. The earlier lead of about 600,000 m3/yr is not supported. [VN-direct] Medium. IND-18, IND-19, IND-20, IND-21.
6. **Feed mills run at about half capacity.** In 2023 there were 269 industrial feed mills with 43.2 Mt/yr design capacity. They produced 20.8 Mt in 2022, about 48% utilisation. Foreign-invested (FDI) firms own 90 of the mills, 51.3% of capacity and 62.5% of output. Spare pelleting capacity for trials of new feed ingredients is widespread. [VN-direct] Medium. IND-39.
7. **Aquafeed has about 5.5 Mt of installed capacity at 60 to 70% utilisation (2025), and a few buyers dominate.** Output was about 920 kt of shrimp feed and 3.0 to 3.9 Mt of fish feed, of which pangasius took 2.0 to 2.5 Mt. Six firms (Sheng Long/Haid, C.P., Uni-President, Tongwei, Grobest, Gromax) supplied 640 kt of shrimp feed, about 70%. A new protein ingredient therefore has about six real buyers in shrimp feed. [VN-direct] Medium (industry estimate). IND-41.
8. **The feed industry consolidated in 2025 and 2026, and new plants target high-protein species.** Cargill left aquafeed in May 2025 and closed its Dong Thap and Tay Ninh (former Long An) plants (trade press). De Heus reportedly took over CJ's feed plants in early 2026 (trade press, unverified). C.P. opened a 124,800 t/yr shrimp-feed plant in Ca Mau in August 2026. De Heus opened an 84,000 t/yr marine and coldwater fish feed plant in Vinh Long in July 2026, for feeds with 40 to 50% protein. [VN-direct] Medium to Low. IND-42, IND-43, IND-45.
9. **One firm dominates soy processing, and we found no protein isolate or concentrate production in Vietnam.** Vinasoy, owned by Quang Ngai Sugar (QNS), has 390 million L/yr of soy milk capacity in three plants (2022). It claims 80% to over 90% of packaged soy milk, depending on the source and segment. We found no soy, pea or rice protein isolate or concentrate plant. Soybeans (2.2 Mt imported in 2024) are crushed or used whole, and isolates appear to be imported (not verified). [VN-direct] Medium. IND-48, IND-49, IND-50.
10. **Do chay (traditional vegetarian mock meat) supply is broad but opaque on capacity.** The September 2026 retail audit logged more than 20 local chay brands. None publishes plant capacity. Large meat and frozen-food processors (Vissan, CJ Cau Tre, Cholimex, SG Food) already run chay lines alongside meat. With the private-label maker for Saigon Co.op, they are the most plausible co-packers for plant-based products. [VN-direct] Medium (presence), Low (capacity). IND-53.
11. **Biologics makers bring GMP bioreactor skills, but their tanks are small and dedicated.** Vietnam has 12 GMP-WHO veterinary vaccine plants making 218 vaccine types, each costing USD 30 to 40 million (December 2024). Dabaco's plant makes 200 million doses/yr. Vabiotech makes a recombinant hepatitis B vaccine expressed in yeast. VNVC's 100 million dose/yr plant in Tay Ninh (former Long An) is due at the end of 2027. For alt protein they are a source of trained staff and quality assurance (QA) systems, not tank volume. [VN-direct] Medium. IND-31, IND-33, IND-34, IND-37.
12. **Cassava glucose syrup is the obvious local carbon source for fermentation, and it is already made at scale.** Between 1994 and 2004 Vedan alone built 140,000 t/yr of glucose syrup, about 216,000 t/yr of native tapioca starch lines and 40,000 t/yr of modified starch (nameplates). It sells glucose syrup to third parties. Daesang runs a starch plant in Tay Ninh. [VN-direct] Medium (old nameplates). IND-04, IND-11.
13. **Public infrastructure exists, but no open pilot-scale food fermentation service was found.** Saigon Hi-Tech Park covers 913 ha, with 165 projects and USD 14.08 bn invested. It has an incubator (SHTP-IC), SHTP Labs and a Biotech Center. FIRI in Hanoi (Ministry of Industry and Trade) offers an ISO/IEC 17025 lab, a microbial culture collection and an experimental production centre. NIFC (Ministry of Health) is the top food control lab. None publishes a 100 L to 1,000 L or larger food-grade fermentation service for startups. [VN-direct] Medium. IND-57, IND-59, IND-60, IND-61.
14. **Plants are designed by foreign engineering firms and fitted out by local contractors.** Examples are Rieckermann (Germany) for the VNVC plant and HTG (Ho Chi Minh City), which did mechanical, electrical and cleanroom work for Ajinomoto. No local maker of GMP-grade fermenters was confirmed in this wave. [VN-direct] Low. IND-31, IND-64.

### 3. Food processing relevant to alt protein

- **Soy.** Vinasoy (QNS) runs plants in Quang Ngai, Bac Ninh and HCMC (former Binh Duong). Capacity is 390 million L/yr (2022). The HCMC (former Binh Duong) plant was designed for 180 million L/yr, with phase 1 of 90 million L opened in December 2017. Soy milk revenue exceeded VND 4,100 bn in 2024. IND-48, IND-49, IND-50.
- **Tofu.** ToFu House (Hung Yen, Van Giang) makes 150 to 500 t/month and is ISO 22000 certified. It is a rare industrial tofu maker in a sector that is otherwise artisanal and was not quantified. Song Long, Pulmuone (Vietnam-made) and Tafu tofu brands were seen on shelves. IND-51, IND-53.
- **Dairy with plant-based lines.** Vinamilk has 9 or more plants in Vietnam. Its Mega factory phase 1 was 400 million L/yr (2013). The field audit logged 17 Vinamilk plant-based products (plant milk, 9-seed yoghurt, nut creamer). TH true NUT, Nutifood/Veyo and Fami/Vinasoy were also on shelf. FrieslandCampina and Lof were not documented. IND-52, IND-53.
- **Meat processors with chay lines.** Vissan (HCMC), CJ Cau Tre (Korean-owned), Cholimex and SG Food. Chay products are made on the same frozen-food lines. The same brand's chay product is often 17 to 38% cheaper than its meat version (field audit). IND-53, IND-55.
- **Do chay specialists** (capacities unknown): An Nhien, Au Lac (Anlac Vegan Food), Thoai An, Top.V (Agrex Saigon), Song Huong Foods (HCMC, Binh Chanh), Xuan Hong, Saky Foods, and private labels made for retailers (Coop Select, reportedly by GN Foods in Tay Ninh; Choice L for Lotte). Imported Chinese canned "Plant Based" luncheon meats (Ong Cha Va, NOSA) sit beside Vissan's local canned chay. IND-53, IND-54.
- **Noodles.** Acecook sells several fully vegan noodle products. Masan had 600 million packs/yr in 2015. Uniben, Vifon and Asia Foods were not documented. IND-30, IND-56.
- **Starch and glucose.** See Vedan and Daesang above. The national cassava starch base (about 120 factories, per a prior lead) belongs to the feedstock agent. Maltodextrin: no Vietnamese producer found (gap).
- **Spray drying, extrusion and protein isolates.** Not established. The only extrusion documented is in aquafeed (for example, Haid's 7 fish-feed extruders). That shows the skills exist, but these are feed-grade lines, not food-grade high-moisture extrusion. No soy, pea or rice protein isolate or concentrate producer was found.
- **OEM and co-packing.** Retailer private labels (Coop Select, Choice L) show that chay OEM already works. Vissan, CJ Cau Tre, Cholimex and SG Food are the likely co-packers for frozen formats. For UHT plant milks, Vinasoy and the dairy majors have spare capacity, but we have no evidence that they sell it to others.

## Implications by audience

- **VCs and investors**
  - Do not underwrite a thesis that assumes rentable aerobic fermentation capacity in Vietnam. There is no published CDMO. Aerobic volume is captive in Vedan, Ajinomoto and Daesang. A first plant will most likely need its own equipment or a structured deal with one incumbent.
  - Incumbents are strategic partners, not just vendors. Vedan (declining Vietnam MSG sales, underused starch and glucose lines, a 120 ha site with its own port) and Ajinomoto (already sells fermentation co-products as feed) have motives to join a JV or host a single-cell protein line.
- **Policy makers**
  - A shared, food-grade pilot fermentation facility (about 50 L to 3,000 L) is a clear public-goods gap. It would fit SHTP's Biotech Center or FIRI's experimental centre, and would benefit from Singapore-style open-access rules.
  - E10 is pulling idle fermentation assets back to fuel ethanol. Policy should not assume those plants are free for protein. It could, however, support ethanol-plant co-products (spent yeast, distillers' grains) as feed protein.
- **Startups**
  - Your shortest routes to scale are: (a) tolling or JV talks with Vedan or Ajinomoto for aerobic fermentation; (b) the frozen-food co-packers that already make chay (Vissan, CJ Cau Tre, Cholimex, SG Food, GN Foods); (c) feed-mill trials, since mills run at about 50% utilisation.
  - For testing, NIFC, FIRI and Eurofins cover nutrition and safety. Check each lab's accreditation scope for novel-protein methods such as amino-acid profile and digestibility (PDCAAS/DIAAS) before relying on it.
- **F&B and feed manufacturers**
  - Six shrimp-feed makers control about 70% of shrimp feed, and new plants target marine fish needing 40 to 50% protein feed. That is the premium buyer segment for local fishmeal substitutes.
  - Ajinomoto and Vedan already sell fermentation-derived feed ingredients, so they are both potential competitors and potential partners for microbial protein.
- **Academia**
  - FIRI's culture collection and Vabiotech's GMP yeast-expression experience are local assets for strain work. Process-scale data (titres, cycle times) from Vietnamese plants are not public. A joint industry-academia process benchmarking study would fill that gap.
- **International bodies**
  - A fundable public good would be a pilot fermentation and extrusion facility with open-access rules, possibly in SHTP. Technical assistance to adapt ethanol or yeast plants for feed-grade single-cell protein under E10 constraints is another.

## Open questions and gaps (cheapest way to close each)

1. **Vedan's current MSG, lysine and fermentation capacity (tank count).** Read Vedan International's 2024 or 2025 annual report on hkexnews.hk (stock code 2317), and Vedan's EIA or environmental permit on thamvan.mae.gov.vn (the site failed to load for us).
2. **Whether any incumbent will toll-ferment.** Direct inquiry to Vedan's Starch Derivatives R&D department and to Ajinomoto Vietnam's corporate planning team. Ask about the 700 m3 tanks and their idle time.
3. **Pilot fermentation access.** Call the SHTP Biotech Center, the HCMC Biotechnology Center and FIRI's experimental production centre. Ask for vessel sizes, food-grade status and startup pricing.
4. **Do chay and plant-based makers' capacity and processes.** Look up each brand's manufacturer on its product labels (the field photos exist), then check the Ministry of Health / VFA self-declaration database and company registration.
5. **Extrusion (TVP and high-moisture), spray drying, maltodextrin and protein isolates.** Customs HS code data for soy protein isolate imports (HS 3504) and TVP (HS 2106.10) would show import dependence. Ask Vinasoy, and the instant coffee spray-dryers (Nestle Tri An, Masan/Vinacafe Bien Hoa, Trung Nguyen), about toll drying.
6. **Citric acid, enzymes, threonine, methionine.** Check customs import data for 2024-2025 and the Vietnam Chemicals Agency plant list.
7. **Hi-tech agriculture zones, Hoa Lac, Da Nang Hi-Tech Park, and university pilot plants.** Read the park websites and the Prime Minister's decisions (e.g., on hi-tech agriculture zone planning) on thuvienphapluat.vn. Pass to the R&D and ecosystem agents.
8. **Fishmeal plants and premix makers.** Ask the Department of Livestock Production and Veterinary (MAE) for the feed facility registry, and VASEP for by-product fishmeal plants.
9. **Current status of the AB Mauri La Nga plant after the 2019 suspension.** Ask the Dong Nai Department of Agriculture and Environment, or AB Mauri directly.
10. **Local stainless fermenter and food-machinery fabricators.** Talk to the Vietnam Mechanical Enterprises Association and to engineering firms that built the Vedan and Ajinomoto expansions.

## Leads for the next wave

- Ajinomoto's 2023 EIA (permit 379/GPMT-BTNMT, 9 October 2023) should list fermenter numbers and volumes. Find the full EIA PDF.
- Vedan sells CMS and PGA broth, and Ajinomoto sells liquid feed ingredients from fermentation broth. These are existing microbial by-product feed streams, with prices and volumes to benchmark single-cell protein against.
- Idle breweries (Heineken Quang Nam) and idle ethanol plants (Binh Phuoc, Phu Tho) could be brownfield sites for fermentation plants: they already have permits, utilities and wastewater treatment.
- Dak To ethanol switched to food-grade ethanol. QNS An Khe plans 30% food-grade. This suggests food-grade fermentation QA exists at small ethanol plants.
- Retail private-label chay (Coop Select, Choice L) shows how an OEM chay supply deal works. Obtain one supplier contract template.
- Vabiotech's recombinant yeast (Hansenula/Pichia) hepatitis B process is the only local GMP precision-fermentation know-how found. Check it for talent and technology transfer to food precision fermentation.
- Skretting and Entobel's insect-meal partnership: how feed majors qualify new proteins (benchmark for single-cell protein).
