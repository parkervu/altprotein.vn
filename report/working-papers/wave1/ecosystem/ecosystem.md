# M4 Ecosystem: who already makes alternative protein in or for Vietnam

Agent: ECOSYSTEM (wave 1). Source prefix: ECO. Date: 23 September 2026.
Data files: `companies.csv` (89 entities), `timeline.csv` (48 events), both in this folder.

**Method note (read first).** This wave used about 40 web searches and about 60 page reads. The session's shared web-search budget ran out part way through. After that I only read pages already found or known company sites. So the directory is broad but not complete. Entities I could not confirm are kept in the CSV and marked "unverified" with confidence Low. Terms: *do chay* (đồ chay) = traditional Vietnamese vegetarian food, including mock meats; *TVP* = textured vegetable (soy) protein; *tau hu ky* (tàu hũ ky) = tofu skin (yuba); *SCP* = single-cell protein (microbial biomass); *BSF* = black soldier fly.

---

## Headline findings

1. **Vietnam's real non-animal protein economy is incumbent, not startup.** Two groups dominate. The first is soy milk: Vinasoy (owned by Quang Ngai Sugar, QNS) reported more than VND 4,100 bn soy-milk revenue in 2024 (about USD 160 million) and about 80% market share. The second is industrial do chay, made by meat and frozen-food processors (VISSAN, CJ Cau Tre, SG Food) and SME specialists (Au Lac, Thoai An, An Nhien). [VN-direct] Medium. ECO-41, ECO-25 to ECO-35, ECO-40.
2. **Much industrial do chay contains little concentrated protein.** CJ Cau Tre's *ha cao chay* (vegetarian dumpling) is 54% wrapper; its filling is jicama, mung bean, taro, mushrooms and glass noodle, with no soy protein listed. VISSAN's *cha gio chay* (spring roll) lists soy protein and tofu skin after five vegetable ingredients. The biggest plant-protein opportunity in food may be upgrading protein in an existing mass category, not creating a new one. [VN-direct] High for label content (primary labels). ECO-33, ECO-40.
3. **Modern branded plant-based meat is almost absent from Vietnamese retail.** Mintel's product-launch database logged **no** plant-based meat launches in Vietnam from 2022 to Q2 2023 (GFI APAC 2023 report). GFI's global company database lists only 2 Vietnamese companies. Our Nha Trang audit (6 September 2026) found no modern meat analogues in frozen aisles. [VN-direct] High. ECO-22, ECO-23, ECO-40.
4. **Imported modern brands entered through restaurants and gourmet shops, not mass retail.** Beyond Burger first appeared at BiaCraft in Ho Chi Minh City (HCMC) in August 2019. OmniFoods ran a Starbucks Vietnam menu in summer 2023. Other imports reached gourmet retailers (Annam Gourmet, Nam An). No official national distributor was found for any brand. [VN-direct] Medium. ECO-15, ECO-16, ECO-20.
5. **The only "Plant Based"-labelled meat analogues found in mass retail are Chinese canned soy luncheon meats** (Ong Cha Va, NOSA). They cost about 3 to 3.3 times more per 100 g than VISSAN's Vietnamese canned *bo nau dau chay* (VND 30,800 to 35,000 vs VND 10,500 per 100 g). [VN-direct] Medium (field prices, some pairings flagged). ECO-40.
6. **Domestic modern startups are few and very small.** Emmay (HCMC, founded 2017, shiitake and mycelium based) sold about 1.5 to 2 t per month in 2023. Exports were about 10% of revenue. Others found: Cay De (VMEAT, VND 3 bn invested 2018 to 2022), Bien Phuong (2020), NutriGreen, Is It Meat? (2020), Kashew Cheese (2020) and Ong Tempeh (about 2021). **I found no disclosed institutional equity round for any Vietnamese plant-based food startup.** [VN-direct] Medium. ECO-12 to ECO-19, ECO-49, ECO-51.
7. **Insect protein is the largest alternative-protein production asset in Vietnam, and the benchmark.** Entobel has 1,000 t/yr in Dong Nai (since 2019) and 10,000 t/yr in HCMC (former Ba Ria-Vung Tau), opened 23 November 2023. It uses HEINEKEN Vietnam by-products as feedstock. Project cost was USD 32.5 million (Mekong Capital USD 25 m, Dragon Capital USD 5 m, IFC USD 2.5 m). It had more than 200 staff in 2026. [VN-direct] High. ECO-01 to ECO-06.
8. **A Vietnamese feed buyer has already signed a large novel-protein offtake.** Vinh Hoan agreed in February 2024 to buy at least 15,000 t of Entobel insect protein over 2025 to 2027. That is about 5,000 t/yr, or about 45% of Entobel's design capacity. Any new feed protein must compete with this incumbent contract. [VN-direct] High. ECO-05.
9. **Chinese SCP is already sold into Vietnamese feed.** A Vietnamese distributor (CK Vietnam / V-Group) imports "Mycoprotein" from China. It is bacterial biomass left over from glutamic acid (MSG) fermentation, with at least 70% crude protein, for livestock, fish and shrimp feed. I found no supply, distributor or trial in Vietnam for Western SCP (Calysta FeedKind, Unibio). Vietnamese aquaculture media covered them only as a global trend (2023 to 2025). The import counterfactual for a Vietnamese SCP play exists today. [VN-direct] Medium. ECO-66 to ECO-69.
10. **Vietnam has no cultivated meat or seafood company or facility.** The only links are corporate. Vinh Hoan took a minority stake in Avant in January 2021 and joined Shiok Meats' Series B in 2021. Minh Phu signed an MOU with Shiok Meats in July 2022 for a joint R&D centre in Vietnam. I found no evidence it went further. Shiok's assets were absorbed by UMAMI Bioworks in March 2024. [VN-direct] High for events, Medium for "no progress". ECO-58 to ECO-63.
11. **Vinh Hoan is the most active Vietnamese corporate in alternative protein.** It invested in cultivated seafood (Avant, Shiok) and buys insect meal (Entobel) through its Singapore venture unit, Vinh Technology. [VN-direct] High. ECO-05, ECO-58, ECO-59.
12. **Microalgae is small and sold as health supplements.** Published figures: Vinh Hao, more than 12,000 m2 and about 17 t/yr (the output figure dates from 2013); VN Vastcom (Nghe An), about 2 ha and about 5 t/yr dry (2023); Dai Viet, 50,000 m2 claimed (Lang Son, Thanh Hoa). One source claims more than 70% of spirulina products sold in Vietnam are imported. No national output total exists. [VN-direct] Medium to Low. ECO-52 to ECO-56.
13. **Plant-based dairy is the largest "modern" category, but much of it is hybrid.** Vinamilk sells soy milk "with fresh milk". TH true NUT contains fresh cow's milk, and its walnut variant has 1 g protein per 100 ml. Fami soy milk has 1.9 g protein per 100 ml. Imports include 137 Degrees (Thai, made by Simple Foods) and Oatside (Singapore brand made in Indonesia, in Vietnam since late 2022). [VN-direct] Medium. ECO-40, ECO-43, ECO-45 to ECO-48.
14. **A large share of the "local" chay shelf is foreign-owned or foreign-made.** Korean CJ owns Cau Tre and bibigo. Retailer private labels include Lotte Mart's Choice L. Canned plant-based items come from China. [VN-direct] Medium. ECO-34, ECO-40.
15. **No commercial duckweed, seaweed-protein, koji or precision-fermentation producer was found in Vietnam.** Tempeh is the only fungal-fermented protein food made commercially that I could verify (Ong Tempeh, HCMC). This is absence of evidence after a truncated search. [VN-direct] Low to Medium. ECO-51, ECO-57.
16. **The support ecosystem is thin and generic.** GFI APAC publishes nothing specific to Vietnam beyond regional surveys. The support I found was women-entrepreneur programmes (ADB #SheExports, Cherie Blair Foundation) for Emmay. The only Vietnamese VC alt-protein deal was VinaCapital Ventures' investment in Korean startup INNOHAS (July 2024). The only development-finance deal was IFC's in Entobel. [VN-direct] Medium. ECO-13, ECO-14, ECO-24, ECO-65, ECO-73.
17. **Published market-size figures for Vietnamese plant-based meat are unreliable.** Press estimates of "about VND 1,000 bn" (2022) and "USD 500 million within several years" (2024) give no traceable basis. [VN-direct] Low. ECO-17 to ECO-19.
18. **One prior lead was wrong.** The "Xuan Hong" found is a cassava starch processor in Tay Ninh (100 t/day, fined in 2020 for bypassing wastewater treatment), not a do chay maker. [VN-direct] Medium. ECO-39.

**Scale is the key gap.** No manufacturer publishes chay tonnage. Label evidence splits the category in two. One type is **vegetable-filler chay** (dumplings, spring rolls): mostly jicama, taro, mung bean and mushroom, with little protein. The other is **protein-analogue chay** (mock meats, *cha lua chay*, canned chay beef): built on soy protein, TVP, tofu skin and probably wheat gluten. The field audit's one numeric label read 4.2 g protein per 100 g for a chay gyoza noodle meal (prior lead, ECO-40). Brief leads I could not verify: Song Huong Foods, LC Foods, Hue One Food, and Tan Nhat Huong (found only as a bakery).

### 3. Plant-based dairy

- **Vinasoy (QNS):** plants in Quang Ngai, Bac Ninh (Tien Son Industrial Park) and HCMC (VSIP 2A, former Binh Duong). Bac Ninh phases 1 and 2 give 180 million L/yr (2015). Soy-milk revenue was more than VND 4,100 bn in 2024, with gross margin above 39% and about 80% market share. The inputs depend on imported soybeans: Vietnam imported 2.2 Mt of soybeans in 2024 for all uses. ECO-41, ECO-42.
- **Vinamilk:** 12 plant-milk SKUs (9-nut, high-protein nut milk with 9 g protein per 240 ml, oat, soy-walnut, soy-red bean), several blended with fresh milk. Nut milk had "double-digit growth" in Q1 2026. ECO-43, ECO-44.
- **TH true NUT:** launched 17 March 2018, using imported macadamia, walnut, oat and dates, plus fresh milk. ECO-45.
- **Nutifood:** brown-rice and corn milks (low protein). ECO-40.
- **Kashew Cheese (2020, HCMC):** fermented cashew cheeses; the only Vietnamese artisanal plant-dairy maker verified. ECO-49, ECO-50.
- **Imports:** 137 Degrees (Thailand, confirmed Thai, answering the brief); Oatside (Singapore brand, made in Bandung, Indonesia; Vietnam launch Nov to Dec 2022; marketing backlash April 2026); Dairy-Free Down Under (Australia). ECO-46 to ECO-48, ECO-15.
- Deep Blue Island and Hat Viet: not found.

### 4. Mushrooms, mycelium, fermentation, SCP

- **Mycelium food:** Emmay only (see §1).
- **Tempeh:** Ong Tempeh (HCMC with a Ha Noi branch, about 2021) makes soy, chickpea and cashew tempeh and sells Rhizopus starter. Vinaorganic advertises tempeh technology transfer (not read). ECO-51.
- **Koji and precision fermentation:** no Vietnamese company or Vietnamese-founded venture found.
- **Feed SCP:** Chinese glutamic-acid-fermentation biomass is sold as "Mycoprotein" by CK Vietnam / V-Group (at least 70% crude protein). A feed trader (Tan Huu Qui) lists "dam don bao" (SCP), but its page could not be read. ECO-69.
- **Mushroom sector:** large (250 to 270 kt/yr per prior project documents), but I found no company turning mushrooms or mycelium into a protein ingredient besides Emmay.

### 5. Microalgae, seaweed, duckweed

- **Spirulina producers found:** Vinh Hao (Lam Dong, former Binh Thuan), Dai Viet (Lang Son, Thanh Hoa), VN Vastcom (Nghe An), Tao Viet cooperative (Ninh Binh), and ECOFARM / MTE (HCMC, former Cu Chi). All sell health food at high prices. Fresh spirulina sells above VND 1.5 m/kg in Ninh Binh (2023, claimed). None is a bulk protein supplier. The Vietnam Academy of Science and Technology (VAST) Institute of Biotechnology is the public technology source. ECO-52 to ECO-56.
- **Seaweed:** no seaweed-protein company examined (search budget exhausted).
- **Duckweed:** 2025 media coverage is generic (citing Wageningen). No Vietnamese producer was found. Research leads (Can Tho University; FiBL with Binca Seafoods 2019) remain unverified. ECO-57.

### 6. Cultivated meat and seafood

See headline 10 and the timeline. Vietnamese press coverage (Nhan Dan 2023, Forbes Vietnam 2022) names no Vietnamese actor. No Vietnamese university cultivated-meat group was found this wave. ECO-64, ECO-72.

### 7. Insect protein (benchmark)

- **Entobel:** see headline 7. Products are H-Meal (at least 53% crude protein), H-Oil and H-Ferti (frass). Design capacity is about 11,000 t/yr of meal. At 53% crude protein, that is at most about 5,800 t/yr of protein (my arithmetic). Actual output is not disclosed. Entobel is preparing to expand into Indonesia. ECO-01 to ECO-06.
- **Cricket One (Dong Nai, former Binh Phuoc):** founded about 2016. Farms are in Loc Ninh (4 company farms and about 20 satellite farms). Feed was developed with Nong Lam University. Funding: a seven-figure USD pre-Series A (2020) and a seven-figure USD Series A (2023). Processing capacity is 1,000 t/yr, with a claimed 10,000 t/yr target. It exports to the EU, North America and Japan. The last evidence of activity is October 2024. ECO-07 to ECO-11.
- Other BSF or cricket firms: not identified (budget). Many farm-scale BSF operations probably exist but were not documented.

### 8. Feed-tech alternative proteins sold in Vietnam

- **Present:** Entobel insect meal (Vinh Hoan offtake); Chinese amino-acid SCP (CK Vietnam / V-Group); yeast products (Angel Yeast has a Vietnam office in Ha Noi, not read in detail); a functional yeast additive (Enzym Group), trialled at ShrimpVet in HCMC. ShrimpVet is a shrimp-trial lab that novel-ingredient makers can use. ECO-69, ECO-70.
- **Not found in Vietnam:** Calysta FeedKind (trials with Thai Union are in Thailand), Unibio, KnipBio, NovoNutrients, Lallemand protein products (not searched). ECO-66, ECO-67.

### 10. Timeline

48 dated events are in `timeline.csv`. Main arcs:
- **1976 to 2015:** incumbents build (spirulina at Vinh Hao, CJ enters, Vinasoy scale-up).
- **2016 to 2020:** micro-startups and imported brands arrive through restaurants (Beyond at BiaCraft in 2019); Entobel starts in Dong Nai (2019); Cricket One raises seed money (2020).
- **2021 to 2022:** corporate cultivated bets (Vinh Hoan in Avant and Shiok; the Minh Phu MOU); Entobel's USD 30 m Series B.
- **2023 to 2024:** Entobel's 10,000 t plant and Vinh Hoan offtake; Cricket One's Series A; GFI records no Vietnamese plant-based launches; Shiok absorbed by UMAMI.
- **2025 to 2026:** SCP appears in Vietnamese feed media; incumbents extend chay and plant-milk lines (VISSAN, August 2026; Vinamilk, Q1 2026).

## Implications by audience

- **VCs and investors:** Vietnam has almost no plant-based deal flow. The investable scale is in insects (Entobel, Cricket One) and in incumbents' chay and soy lines. A thesis built on "upgrading protein in do chay" would sell to VISSAN, CJ or SG Food as buyers or acquirers, not to consumers. The only recent Vietnamese VC alt-protein cheque (VinaCapital Ventures) went to a Korean company.
- **Policy makers:** The existing alt-protein economy (do chay, soy milk, spirulina, insect meal) operates under current food and feed law. New categories (mycelium, SCP for food, cultivated) have no Vietnamese applicant to test the rules. Imports of Chinese SCP for feed already enter the market, so feed rules are live today.
- **Startups:** Industrial do chay makers are the natural co-manufacturers and first customers for protein ingredients (TVP, mycoprotein, tofu-skin upgrades). The label evidence shows headroom: many SKUs contain little protein. Entobel's Vinh Hoan contract sets the reference for feed offtake terms.
- **F&B and feed manufacturers:** Chay versions sell 17 to 38% cheaper than the same brand's meat versions (field audit), so plant-based already competes on price in Vietnam. The gap is protein content and nutrition labelling. Feed mills already have two alternative-protein suppliers (insect meal, Chinese SCP) to benchmark against.
- **Academia:** There are clear gaps in measured protein content of do chay, spirulina output statistics, and duckweed or seaweed protein. Existing links are Nong Lam University (insect feed) and VAST (algae).
- **International bodies:** Vietnam is largely missing from GFI APAC's work. A Vietnam company census and a do chay nutrition audit would be low-cost public goods.

## Open questions and gaps (with the cheapest way to close each)

1. **Chay tonnage and protein inputs by manufacturer.** Call VISSAN, CJ Foods Vietnam, SG Food, Au Lac and Thoai An. Also check the General Statistics Office (now NSO) enterprise census for the relevant product codes.
2. **TVP, soy protein isolate and wheat gluten import volumes** (HS 2106.10, 3504, 1109). Check Vietnam Customs statistics or trade-data services (ImportGenius, Panjiva). This decides whether do chay protein is domestic or imported.
3. **Chinese SCP import volumes and manufacturers.** Check customs HS 2309 and 2102 lines; call CK Vietnam / V-Group.
4. **Cricket One status in 2025 and 2026.** Check LinkedIn or call the company, and check the Dong Nai business registry.
5. **Brief leads not found:** Song Huong Foods, LC Foods, Hue One Food, Tan Nhat Huong chay, Deep Blue Island, Hat Viet, Zhenmeat, "Unconventional". A new search round with a fresh search budget should close these.
6. **The 2 Vietnamese companies in GFI's database.** Email GFI APAC or download the public spreadsheet.
7. **NGO and event ecosystem** (HSI Forward Food trainings, ProVeg, vegetarian associations, plant-based expos, university startup contests): not covered. Needs a new search round.
8. **Duckweed, seaweed and koji actors:** not covered beyond absence. Check Can Tho University, Nha Trang University (seaweed) and FiBL.
9. **Status of the Minh Phu-UMAMI (ex-Shiok) MOU.** Check Minh Phu investor relations or annual reports for 2023 to 2025.
