# Brief GEO: geopolitics, trade, disease, energy and demography to 2050

Folder: `research/wave3/geo_macro/` · Source ID prefix: **GEO** · WebSearch cap: **15** · Question tree: F1, F5.

Edition 1.0 context: ch01-why-vietnam (import dependence, 2022 and 2026 shocks), ch08-regional (China scale), ch13-outlook (milestones, UN population, PDP8). Read those first.

## Sub-questions
1. **Trade and geopolitics.** Risks to protein-input trade to 2050: chokepoints and shipping, US and China tensions, US tariffs on Vietnam (2025 to 2026 status), export restrictions on grains and oilseeds (history and likelihood), supply growth projections for Brazil and Argentina soy and maize, EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) timing and effect on soy trade, CBAM scope and any plan to extend to agriculture, trade agreements (CPTPP, EVFTA, RCEP) relevant to protein inputs and alt-protein exports.
2. **China's protein and feed strategy to 2035** (soybean-meal reduction plans, single-cell protein scale, "greater food" approach, 15th Five-Year Plan text on biomanufacturing): implications for Vietnam as supplier (starch, fishmeal) and competitor.
3. **Animal disease and pandemic risk:** African swine fever (history in Vietnam, vaccine status), highly pathogenic avian influenza including H5N1 in mammals and dairy cattle since 2024, antimicrobial resistance and Vietnam's livestock antibiotic-reduction targets, zoonotic spillover risk, frequency trends; quantify historical supply shocks (e.g. ASF 2019 herd loss).
4. **Energy transition to 2050:** revised PDP8 (Decision 768/QD-TTg 2025) 2050 mix, offshore wind, nuclear restart, LNG dependence, electricity price trajectory; energy security implications for fermentation.
5. **Demography and income to 2050:** UN WPP 2024 (population, ageing, urbanisation, working-age share), GDP per capita paths (Vietnam's 2045 high-income goal; World Bank "Vietnam 2045" report), implications for protein demand (macro only) and labour for farms and plants; automation.
6. **Workforce and just transition:** number of livestock and aquaculture households and workers, smallholder share, trends to 2050; what a shift in protein supply means for them.
