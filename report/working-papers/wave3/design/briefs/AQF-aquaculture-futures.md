# Brief AQF (wave 4 gap): aquaculture, marine ingredients and protein-crop futures to 2050

Folder: `research/wave3/aquaculture_futures/` · Source ID prefix: **AQF** · WebSearch cap: **12** · Question tree: F1, F4.

Why: aquafeed is the market where alternative feed proteins fit best (edition 1.0), yet no dated aquaculture projections beyond 2035 were found, trash fish must be replaced by formulated feed as capture fisheries shrink (Decision 339: capture 2.8 Mt by 2030), and 2026 shows an anchoveta crisis. Read first: `wave3/balance_model/balance_model.md`, `wave3/climate/climate.md` (sections on aquaculture and fishmeal), `wave3/horizon_scan/horizon_scan.md` (anchoveta signal), `wave3/national_targets/national_targets.md` (fisheries targets), and edition 1.0 `content/03-appendices/app-h-feed-market.md`.

## Sub-questions
1. **Aquaculture trajectories to 2040 and 2050** for Vietnam: whiteleg and black tiger shrimp, pangasius, marine finfish (e.g. cobia, pompano, grouper in offshore cages; Vietnam's offshore aquaculture plans), tilapia, snakehead. Published projections (FAO SOFIA 2024 and its 2032 projections, OECD-FAO, WorldFish or IFPRI fish models, Vietnamese strategy documents), and the drivers (climate, salinity, export markets, disease).
2. **Trash fish and low-value fish in feed:** current use in Vietnam (tonnes, species groups), the policy to replace it with formulated feed, and the implied increase in formulated aquafeed and protein demand to 2030 and 2040.
3. **Marine ingredients to 2050:** global fishmeal and fish oil supply projections, share from trimmings and by-products, Vietnam's own fishmeal production from pangasius and seafood by-products; omega-3 (EPA and DHA) supply gap and alternatives (microalgae oil, e.g. Veramaris; GM oilseeds), which matter as much as protein for marine species.
4. **Protein crops for Vietnam to 2050:** soybean and other legumes (varieties, yields, area trends, gene editing or GM status in Vietnam, e.g. approved GM soybean and maize events), use of released rice land, intercropping; duckweed and azolla in integrated systems; realistic domestic protein contribution by 2050.
5. **Offshore and recirculating aquaculture:** plans and signals (offshore marine aquaculture targets, RAS projects) and their feed implications.
6. **Implications [estimate]:** 2050 aquafeed protein demand range and the share alternative ingredients could credibly take, separately for protein and for omega-3.
