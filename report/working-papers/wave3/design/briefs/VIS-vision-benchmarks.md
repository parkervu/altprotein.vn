# Brief VIS (wave 5 gap): protein-transition visions and roadmaps elsewhere, as benchmarks for a Vietnam 2050 vision

Folder: `research/wave3/vision_benchmarks/` · Source ID prefix: **VIS** · WebSearch cap: **12** · Question tree: F9, F10.

Why: Part V will include one normative "Vietnam 2050" protein-supply vision with backcasting milestones. It should be anchored in what other countries have actually committed to, funded and achieved, and in good foresight practice, not invented. Read first: `wave3/econ_policy/econ_policy.md` (bioeconomy strategies; do not repeat its budgets), `wave3/national_targets/national_targets.md`, and edition 1.0 `content/01-report/ch08-regional.md` and `ch13-outlook.md`.

## Sub-questions
1. **National protein-transition strategies with quantified goals:** e.g. Denmark (Action Plan for Plant-based Foods 2023 and the plant-based fund), the Netherlands (National Protein Strategy 2020; 50:50 animal to plant protein goal), Germany or the UK, Canada (Protein Industries Canada), Israel (alternative-protein roadmap and incentives), Singapore (Food Story and its revision), Korea (food tech master plan), Japan (food tech and green food system strategy MIDORI), China ("greater food" approach, feed soybean-meal reduction), Thailand (future food), EU (protein plan, 2023 to 2026 debates). For each: year, horizon, targets (self-sufficiency, plant-to-animal ratios, production volumes, funding), what has been achieved or missed so far, and lessons.
2. **Feed-protein self-sufficiency strategies:** EU protein plan (2018 report), China's soybean-meal reduction, Japan's feed self-sufficiency targets, Korea: what targets, what levers, what results.
3. **Foresight practice for food systems:** how national food-system foresight exercises built normative visions and backcasting (e.g. FAO's "Future of food and agriculture" alternative pathways, the UK Foresight "Future of Food and Farming" 2011, Singapore Centre for Strategic Futures, Finland's Sitra, Thailand's NXPO foresight, the EU JRC food 2035 or 2040 scenarios, Vietnam's own foresight or "Vision 2045" exercises). Extract the methods that worked: horizon, number of scenarios, backcasting milestones, signposts, review cycles.
4. **What credible ambitious targets look like** for a middle-income, feed-importing, aquaculture-exporting country by 2040 or 2050 (use analogues such as Thailand, China, Brazil, Chile or Norway for aquafeed; Denmark for food), expressed as ranges, with the analogues cited.
