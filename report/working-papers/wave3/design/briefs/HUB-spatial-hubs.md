# Brief HUB (wave 4 gap): where a 2040 protein bioeconomy could sit in Vietnam

Folder: `research/wave3/spatial_hubs/` · Source ID prefix: **HUB** · WebSearch cap: **12** · Question tree: F3, F6, F9.

Why: investors, startups and provinces need to know where frontier inputs will cluster: renewable power and hydrogen, CO2, carbohydrate, aquafeed buyers, industrial parks and research. Read first: `wave3/frontier_gas/co2_point_sources_vn.csv` and `vn_energy_inputs_2050.csv`, `wave3/national_targets/national_targets_2050.csv`, `wave3/frontier_bio/frontier_bio.md` (MAE biotech clusters, Directive 13/CT-TTg), and edition 1.0 `data/facilities.csv`, `data/provinces.csv`, `content/03-appendices/app-p-admin-map.md`. Use current (post-July 2025) province names with former units in brackets.

## Sub-questions
1. **Energy and hydrogen geography:** provinces with offshore wind zones, planned hydrogen or green-ammonia projects and hubs (e.g. Vinh Long (former Tra Vinh), Can Tho (former Soc Trang), Khanh Hoa (former Ninh Thuan), Lam Dong (former Binh Thuan)), renewable energy centres named in the revised PDP8 and regional plans, planned transmission.
2. **Bio and high-tech zones:** high-tech parks, high-tech agriculture zones (and the 2026 decree on them), the MAE biotechnology zones and clusters due by December 2026, industrial parks with bio or food focus, planned "free trade zones" or special economic zones that could host biomanufacturing.
3. **Carbohydrate and residue clusters:** starch, sugar, brewing, rice-straw and seafood-processing concentrations by current province (from edition 1.0 data plus any new sources).
4. **Buyer clusters:** aquafeed and livestock-feed mills by province; shrimp and pangasius production by province.
5. **Candidate hubs [estimate]:** combine the layers into 4 to 6 candidate hub regions for 2035 to 2045 (e.g. a Mekong aquafeed-and-hydrogen hub; a Tay Ninh cassava fermentation-sugar hub; a Ho Chi Minh City and Dong Nai tolling and pilot hub; a south-central renewable-protein coast; a northern research hub around Hanoi and Hoa Lac). For each: assets, missing pieces, which plays (edition 1.0 T1 to T10 and frontier routes) fit, and signposts.

## CSV outputs
- `hub_layers.csv`: layer (energy, hydrogen, co2, bio_zone, carbohydrate, residue, buyer, research), item, province_current, province_former, status (operating, planned, proposed), capacity_or_size, horizon_year, source_ids, evidence_label, confidence, notes.
- `candidate_hubs.csv`: hub, provinces, assets, missing_pieces, fitting_plays, horizon, signposts, source_ids, confidence, notes.
