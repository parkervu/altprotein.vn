# Brief CLM: climate and environment drivers to 2050

Folder: `research/wave3/climate/` · Source ID prefix: **CLM** · WebSearch cap: **14** · Question tree: F1, F3.

Edition 1.0 context: ch01-why-vietnam (livestock 5.8% of GHG in 2016; aquaculture scale), ch02-asset-map (cassava, rice), ch09-economics (tropical cooling penalty), ch13-outlook.

## Sub-questions
1. **Vietnam climate projections** (official scenarios, e.g. the 2020 or later climate change scenarios of the ministry now MAE; IPCC AR6 SSP-based studies): temperature, sea-level rise by 2050 and 2100, Mekong Delta salinity intrusion, land subsidence rates (e.g. Minderhoud et al.), share of delta land at risk; extreme heat and wet-bulb temperature trends.
2. **Aquaculture impacts** (shrimp, pangasius): heat, salinity, disease, water quality; projected production effects; adaptation (shrimp-rice systems, relocation, recirculating systems).
3. **Livestock impacts:** heat stress on pigs and poultry, productivity losses, disease range shifts.
4. **Domestic crop impacts:** rice (yield and area, rice land conversion in the delta), cassava (climate suitability, drought tolerance, disease such as cassava mosaic), maize, sugarcane. Implications for Vietnam's carbon feedstocks for fermentation.
5. **Imported feed inputs:** climate-driven yield volatility for soybean and maize in Brazil, Argentina and the United States to 2050; fishmeal: Peruvian anchoveta and El Niño frequency under warming. Quantify where the literature does.
6. **Emissions constraints:** Vietnam's net-zero 2050 commitment, NDC updates (check whether NDC 3.0 was submitted in 2025 or 2026 and what it says for agriculture), the Global Methane Pledge, the national climate strategy (Decision 896/QD-TTg 2022) agriculture targets, livestock and aquaculture emissions trajectories, and what these imply for protein supply choices.
7. **Water:** Mekong upstream dams, groundwater depletion, water stress for fermentation siting and aquaculture.
8. **Warming and fermentation cooling:** projected change in design wet-bulb temperatures in southern Vietnam by 2050 and what it does to the cooling penalty (edition 1.0 estimated USD 70 to 180 per tonne of biomass today).
