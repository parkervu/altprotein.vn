# Brief FTB: bio-design frontier: AI, synthetic biology, precision fermentation, cultivated, molecular farming (2026 to 2050)

Folder: `research/wave3/frontier_bio/` · Source ID prefix: **FTB** · WebSearch cap: **15** · Question tree: F2, F7.

Edition 1.0 context: ch10-technology-fit, app-j-science (titers, cell lines, duckweed, cost benchmarks), ch05-rules (GMO Decree 43/2026), ch06-knowledge-talent. Read those first; do not repeat them.

## Sub-questions
1. **AI for protein and strain design.** What is the evidence (not the hype) that AI accelerates alternative-protein R&D: protein language models and structure prediction, generative enzyme design, machine-learning media and process optimisation, self-driving labs. Use Rethink Priorities' AI and cultivated meat research (https://rethinkpriorities.org/research-area/ai-and-cultivated-meat/) and peer-reviewed studies. Quantify acceleration where studies do (e.g. fewer design-build-test cycles, cost reductions in media). Separate demonstrated effects from claims.
2. **Biofoundries and cloud labs.** Global Biofoundry Alliance members in Asia; access models and costs; could Vietnamese teams use them remotely. Vietnam's AI and compute capacity relevant to bio-design (national AI strategy, AI law status, Viettel, FPT, VinAI or others, supercomputers), and biotech training.
3. **Precision fermentation to 2050.** Titer and productivity trends, continuous processing, downstream cost, published cost-parity estimates and dates (label company or advocacy figures as claims), global approvals trend, host strains (Pichia, Trichoderma, Aspergillus, Kluyveromyces), IP landscape (key patents expiring).
4. **Cultivated meat and seafood to 2050.** Updated TEAs (2023 to 2026), cell-density and media-cost records, food-grade media, bioreactor scale, approvals count by country by year, investment trend and notable closures, cultivated seafood players in Asia (e.g. Umami Bioworks, formerly Shiok and merged with others), realistic parity dates in the literature, and what a 2040 or 2050 production system could look like.
5. **Molecular farming.** Plant-made animal proteins (e.g. Moolec, Alpine Bio, PoLoPo, Kyomei, Nobell): approvals (USDA APHIS "Am I Regulated", FDA), yields per hectare of target protein, relevance to Vietnam (rice, soybean, duckweed or cassava as hosts; Decree 43/2026 GM crop rules).
6. **Cell-free protein synthesis** for food: status, costs, realistic horizon.
7. **Next-generation biomass fermentation** (continuous mycoprotein, side-stream fungi) and **hybrid products**: trends, closures and pivots 2024 to 2026.
8. **For each family:** Vietnam window (when, under what conditions), capability gaps, and a plausible role for Vietnam in 2040 and 2050 (label as estimate or vision where speculative).
