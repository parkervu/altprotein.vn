# Vietnam's long-horizon targets and plans relevant to protein supply (2030 to 2050)

Stream: NTS (national targets). Wave 3, futures round. Accessed 2026-09-24. Question tree: F5, F9.
Status: complete for this round (24 Sep 2026). Data file: `national_targets_2050.csv` (98 rows).

Scope: every official target, direction or task for 2030, 2035, 2045 and 2050 that bears on protein supply, feed, biotechnology and biomanufacturing, food systems, land, water, energy inputs and climate. Official targets are foresight type [projection] (official target) unless marked otherwise. Our own arithmetic is marked [estimate].

Reused from other wave 3 streams (cross-checked, not re-researched): climate caps (CLM, `wave3/climate/emissions_targets.csv`), power and hydrogen (FTG, `wave3/frontier_gas/vn_energy_inputs_2050.csv`), AI strategy and biotech drafting (FTB, `wave3/frontier_bio/frontier_bio.md`).

## Headline findings

1. **Official targets for protein-supply volumes stop at 2030.** The last numbers are meat 6.0 to 6.5 Mt (Decision 1520), industrial livestock feed 30 to 32 Mt (Decisions 1520 and 1625), aquaculture 7.0 Mt and capture 2.8 Mt (Decision 339). The 2045 visions for livestock and fisheries are qualitative. We found no official meat, feed, fishmeal, feed-crop or alternative-protein number for 2035, 2040, 2045 or 2050. [VN-direct] High (absence checked in 1520, 339, 150, 1748, 1625, 1466); projection (official target); 2030. NTS-07, NTS-08, NTS-22, NTS-23.
2. **Inputs are planned to 2050, outputs are not.** Non-hydro renewables 74 to 75% of power, 240 GW of offshore wind for hydrogen and ammonia, 10 to 20 Mt of clean hydrogen a year, an agriculture emissions cap of 56 MtCO2e, water demand of 131.7 billion m3 and net zero are all official 2050 numbers. A 2050 protein vision can anchor on these, but has no official protein or feed anchor. [VN-direct] High; projection; 2050. NTS-24 to NTS-26, NTS-29, FTG-15, FTG-16, CLM-01.
3. **New: the first official 2035 biotech targets for agriculture.** Decision 1466/QD-TTg (31 Jul 2026) replaces the 2021 agri-biotech scheme. By 2030 it targets "thay thế ít nhất 50% sản phẩm công nghệ sinh học nhập khẩu" and 7% of biotech industry value added. For 2031 to 2035, with a vision to 2045, it targets 60% import replacement and 15% of biotech value added. It names "công nghệ enzym, protein, vi sinh" for nutrient-use efficiency in livestock and aquaculture, but not alternative protein, new food, precision fermentation or microbial biomass. [VN-direct] Medium; projection; 2030, 2035, 2045. NTS-35.
4. **Three different import-replacement targets apply to 2030, and none can be measured.** Resolution 36 and Decision 1466 say at least 50% of biotech imports. The MOIT scheme (Decision 1355/QD-BCT, June 2026) says at least 20% for scheme products, rising to 30 to 50% by 2045. The feed scheme (Decision 1625) says domestic additives meet 30 to 35% of demand. None defines a baseline or product basket. [VN-direct] Medium; projection; 2030, 2045. NTS-01, NTS-23, NTS-35, NTS-38.
5. **Resolution 36's 2045 goal of biotech at 10 to 15% of GDP has no statistical definition.** Neither it nor the 2030 goal of 7% of GDP can be tracked from published statistics. Do not confuse either with Decision 1466's "15% of biotech industry value added". [VN-direct] High; projection; 2045. NTS-01, NTS-35.
6. **Rice land is planned to fall by about 17% in six years.** The adjusted national land-use plan (Decision 1177/QD-TTg, 30 Jun 2026) sets rice land at 3,252.63 thousand ha for 2030, against 3,907.07 thousand ha in 2024 and 3,568.48 thousand ha in the 2021 plan. That releases about 654 thousand ha (our calculation). The crop strategy sets no maize, soybean or cassava target and no aim to cut feed imports. [VN-direct] Medium; projection; 2030. NTS-15, NTS-22.
7. **The feed scheme, which edition 1.0 missed, clarifies the 30 to 32 Mt target.** Decision 1625/QD-TTg (Dec 2023) targets 24 to 25 Mt of industrial livestock feed in 2025 and 30 to 32 Mt in 2030, which answers the edition 1.0 scope question: the target appears to cover livestock feed only, not aquafeed. Domestic feed additives are to meet 30 to 35% of demand, with VND 1,310 billion (about USD 50 million), about VND 700 billion of it for by-product processing. The 2025 figure looks missed (about 21.5 Mt in 2024). [VN-direct] Medium; projection; 2025, 2030. NTS-23.
8. **Rice straw becomes a large official side stream.** On the 1 million ha low-emission rice programme, 100% of straw is to be collected from the fields and reprocessed by 2030. Our rough estimate is 7 to 15 Mt of straw a year, which biomass power will also compete for (4.8 to 7.0 GW planned by 2050). [VN-direct] Medium for targets; [estimate] Low; 2030, 2050. NTS-32, NTS-26.
9. **Agriculture's climate caps are tight, and the official levers do not include alternative protein.** Emissions are capped at 64 MtCO2e in 2030 and 56 MtCO2e in 2050. Methane is to fall 30% from 2020 by 2030 and a further 40% by 2050 (we read the 2050 base as 2030). The official livestock levers are feed rations, manure and biogas. NDC 3.0 (2026 to 2035) was still not approved or submitted on 24 Sep 2026. [VN-direct] High for caps, Medium for the methane base; projection; 2030, 2050. NTS-24, NTS-27, CLM-01.
10. **The official income path is steep: use it as a high case.** Resolution 252/2025/QH15 targets growth of 10% a year or more in 2026 to 2030, and GDP per person of about USD 8,500 in 2030 and USD 38,000 in 2050 (current prices), up from USD 7,500 and USD 27,000 to 32,000 in the 2023 plan. That implies about 8.5% a year in current-dollar terms for 25 years (our arithmetic). [VN-direct] Medium; projection and estimate; 2030, 2050. NTS-13, NTS-14.
11. **There is no official protein-intake norm.** The nutrition strategy (Decision 02/2022), the health resolution (72-NQ/TW, 2025) and the food-systems plan (Decision 300/2023) set targets for height, stunting, overweight, salt and life expectancy (above 80 years by 2045). None sets a protein target or mentions plant-based or new foods. [VN-direct] Medium; projection; 2030, 2045. NTS-10, NTS-16, NTS-17.
12. **Strategic technology missions leave out fermentation.** Decision 808/QD-TTg (May 2026) sets 20 missions to 2030. The agricultural ones cover gene-edited breeds and new veterinary vaccines, with no mission on fermentation, microbial protein or food biomanufacturing. The national biotechnology strategy for 2026 to 2030 (vision 2045) was still a draft. [VN-direct] Medium; signal; 2030. NTS-20, FTB-49.
13. **Energy security points to market prices.** Resolution 70-NQ/TW (Aug 2025) raises renewables to 25 to 30% of primary energy by 2030, well above the 15 to 20% in the green growth strategy, and orders market-based energy prices without cross-subsidy. It also calls for hydrogen and ammonia pilots linked to offshore wind. [VN-direct] Medium; projection and signal; 2030, 2045. NTS-19.
14. **The 2025 reforms rewrote the planning layer but not the sector strategies.** The master plan was amended (Res 252/2025), the land plan adjusted (Decision 1177/2026), the S&T strategy adjusted (Decision 604/2026) and the agri-biotech scheme replaced (Decision 1466/2026), and Resolution 19 was continued (Conclusion 219-KL/TW; Res 85/NQ-CP). The livestock, fisheries, crop and climate strategies are unchanged, and their MARD and MONRE tasks now sit with MAE. [VN-direct] Medium; trend. NTS-05, NTS-14, NTS-15, NTS-21, NTS-35.
15. **Capture fisheries must shrink by about a quarter while aquaculture grows.** Moving from 3.83 Mt in 2025 (QNT) to 2.8 Mt in 2030, with aquaculture at 7.0 Mt and a directive to replace trash fish with industrial feed ("thay thế việc sử dụng cá tạp"), raises the need for formulated aquafeed protein. No official target covers aquafeed or fishmeal. [VN-direct] Medium; projection and estimate; 2030. NTS-08.
16. **The next windows to add protein and feed targets fall in 2026 to 2030:** the MAE biotech zones and clusters plan (due December 2026), the national biotechnology strategy (in draft), NDC 3.0, the new water strategy to 2050 (2026), and the successor livestock and fisheries strategies (due before the current ones expire in 2030). [VN-direct] Medium; signal; 2026 to 2030. NTS-05, NTS-37, FTB-49.

### C. Livestock and fisheries to 2045 (F5)

- **Livestock (Dec 1520/QD-TTg, 6 Oct 2020), 2045 vision:** "chăn nuôi Việt Nam là ngành kinh tế kỹ thuật hiện đại"; capacity "thuộc nhóm đầu của các nước khu vực Đông Nam Á"; "trên 70% khối lượng sản phẩm chăn nuôi chính được qua sơ chế, chế biến công nghiệp, trong đó khoảng 30% được chế biến sâu"; 100% of marketed meat from industrial slaughterhouses. **No 2045 meat, feed or per-capita number is set.** The by-product list for feed (spent grain, brewer's yeast, pineapple and cassava pulp, blood and slaughter by-products, shrimp heads and shells, pangasius bone and fat) is the most concrete official mandate for side-stream feed protein. NTS-07 [VN-direct] Medium.
- **Fisheries (Dec 339/QD-TTg, 11 Mar 2021):** 2030: total output 9.8 Mt; aquaculture 7.0 Mt; capture 2.8 Mt with "Giảm dần cường lực khai thác"; exports USD 14 to 16 billion; production value +3 to 4% a year; post-harvest losses below 10%. Aquafeed: "Chủ động sản xuất thức ăn công nghiệp thay thế việc sử dụng cá tạp" (industrial feed to replace trash fish). 2045: "thuộc nhóm ba nước sản xuất và xuất khẩu thủy sản dẫn đầu thế giới"; a centre of deep processing. **No 2045 volume, no fishmeal or feed-protein target.** NTS-08 [VN-direct] Medium.
- Reading: official volume targets stop at 2030 for meat, feed and aquaculture. For 2040 and 2050 the balance model must use its own scenarios, anchored on the 2030 targets. [VN-direct] High (absence confirmed in three texts); [estimate] for the implication.

Reading. The state plans the **inputs** of a future protein system (clean power, hydrogen, carbon limits, water, land) to 2050, but plans the **outputs** (meat, feed, aquaculture, feed crops) only to 2030, and never plans protein itself. The first official 2035 numbers relevant to protein supply are the biotech import-replacement and value-added targets in Decision 1466 (2026). A 2050 vision chapter therefore has no official protein or feed anchor to backcast from; it must build its own, anchored on the 2030 targets and the 2050 input plans. ## Implications by audience
- **Investors:** Decision 1466 gives an official 2035 import-replacement target (60%) for agri biotech products, which supports domestic enzymes, probiotics, feed additives and microbial products; it does not create demand for alternative protein as such. Energy and hydrogen are the only long-horizon input plans relevant to gas fermentation; treat them as options, not commitments (FTG shows slippage). [VN-direct] Medium.
- **Policy makers:** the planning stack has a gap between 2030 and 2050 for protein and feed. The next windows are the national biotech strategy, the MAE cluster plan (December 2026), NDC 3.0 and the successor livestock and fisheries strategies. Each could carry a protein-and-feed balance target and define the import basket for the biotech targets. [VN-direct] High for the gap; [estimate] for the options.
- **Startups:** the 1466 line on "công nghệ enzym, protein, vi sinh" for nutrient-use efficiency, and the by-product valorisation lines in 1625 (about VND 700 billion) and 1466, are the official hooks for feed-protein ventures. The start-up strategy (Res 86) sets venture targets but does not prioritise biotech. [VN-direct] Medium.
- **F&B and feed manufacturers:** the official path is more feed (30 to 32 Mt by 2030), less capture fish (2.8 Mt), 650 thousand ha less rice land and 100% straw collection on 1 million ha, with no official target for domestic maize or soybean. Protein import dependence is therefore not addressed by any official volume target. [VN-direct] Medium; [estimate] for the reading.
- **Research bodies:** talent targets are small relative to the ambition: 500 high-level agri-biotech staff and 2,000 technician trainings by 2030 (1466), 12 FTE researchers per 10,000 people (Res 57). No biofoundry or bioprocess pilot-plant target exists. [VN-direct] Medium.
- **International bodies:** support is most leveraged where official targets exist but cannot be measured: statistics for biotech value added and import baskets, agricultural pathways in NDC 3.0, and the 2050 water strategy for the Mekong. [VN-direct] Medium.
